# Station Zero: creative bible and build plan

This folder is the creative bible and the build plan of the custom Zombies map Station Zero, written on 10-11 November 2026 and reviewed twice; the last round of fixes was not re-read.
Reading order: `00-PITCH.md`, `01-LORE.md` (the canon; `wiki/` retells it in the fiction's own voice, 40 pages), `02-WORLD.md`, then `03` to `11` by subject (transport, Pack-a-Punch quest, wonder weapons, perks, enemies, camos, tab and HUD, emotes, audio direction).
Then `13-RISKS.md`, and `REVIEW.md` with `review/` (the two reviews and the changes made after them).
`12-BUILD-PLAN.md` is the work order: the packages, their order and what proves each one.
`14-CLIENT-CAPABILITIES.md` says what each feature needs from the game client, and which of those the client does not do yet.
`research/` holds the measurements and tool readiness notes the chapters cite (scripts in `research/E-scripts/`).
Raw game-data dumps and binaries are not kept here; where a research note names a dump, it lives outside the repository.
Machine paths in the notes are written as `<runtime>`, `<server>`, `<products>`, `<temp>`.
Nothing of it is built yet: this is design and plan only, and no number in it is a measured result of a Station Zero build.
