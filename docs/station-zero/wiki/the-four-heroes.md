# The Four Heroes

STATION ZERO follows four people into the station on the night of 31 December 1985, a year to the day after the [Midnight Service](the-incident.md). In the film they are cast as four city archetypes: a Transit Cop, a Graffiti King, a Signal Engineer and a Night Desk Reporter. The players are the four actors of Willard Wyler's regular company, who find themselves re-cast in the picture. Each has a reason to be there tonight, and none has told the others the real one.

## The Transit Cop (A.J.)

A patrol officer of the Rapid's Transit Police, posted to Station Zero's plaza detail. On 1 January 1985 at 00:07 he reported "smell of gas", as a Lodestar liaison had instructed, and signed the incident report to that effect. He has been posted at the Cordon ever since, guarding a barrier against a leak that never existed. Tonight the turnstiles started clicking by themselves, and he went down to prove to himself that there is nothing there.

**Costume.** Dark uniform, with shoulder patches reading "HRT TRANSIT POLICE · PORT HALCYON", a duty belt and a flashlight.

## The Graffiti King (Andre)

A street painter whose name is on forty walls in Port Halcyon, and the only Graffiti King there is. The Zero Kings' work on the city's trains stopped a year ago, and the story on the street is that a whole clean train is sitting sealed under the plaza: the last unpainted train in the city. For eleven months he looked for a way in. Tonight he came through the storm drain, with a rucksack of cans, to paint it top to bottom.

**Costume.** Paint-stained workwear, with a respirator hanging at the neck.

## The Signal Engineer (Poindexter)

A junior signal technician in the Rapid's Signals Department. For a year the network diagram in his depot has shown a train "approaching" at a station that officially has no power. Nobody above him would hear of it, and on the last night of the year he was sent, alone, with a clipboard, to pull one fuse and sign the sheet.

**Costume.** A coverall carrying the Rapid's disc on the chest, a clipboard, and a tool roll.

## The Night Desk Reporter (Sally)

A Channel 9 Night Desk reporter, sent to the barrier to do a one-year-after piece. Her cameraman, Lou Fenwick, went under the canopy to get a better shot and did not come back. She followed, with the microphone still live.

**Costume.** A raincoat, and a handheld microphone whose flag reads "9 NIGHT DESK".

## Why the film needs four

Wyler casts four of everything. Each hero comes by a separate door of the story: the officer from the street, the painter from the drain, the engineer from the depot and the reporter from the van. The first reel cuts between the four entrances and brings them together at the first chime of the Three-Tone.

## In the map

The four heroes are the player characters and begin in the Cordon, under the quarantine canopy, beside the Channel 9 van. The Graffiti King's storm drain is the culvert in the Sump, with a rope still hanging from its bent grille. The Engineer's fuse boxes stand in Platform A West, the Car Barn and the Converter Hall. The Cop's own post is the Transit Police Post.
