# The Dead

**The dead** of Station Zero are the passengers, staff and cleaners lost to the Midnight Service and its aftermath: 312 people aboard the 23:47 (308 guests and a crew of 4), 58 night staff of the station, and about 140 Lodestar cleanup workers sent in over the following year. Together that is about 510 people.

## What they are not

They are not infected, and nothing spreads by bite. They do not hunger. They are not malicious. The Lodestar cleaners' logs, which are the nearest thing to a field study, say of them: "They are not hunting. They are late."

## Why they died

The night staff on the concourse died at 23:47 because the chime reached them. The Three-Tone sounds on every public address horn, and it reaches everyone in earshot of one: the passengers on the 23:47, the 58 night staff on the concourse and platforms, and, over the following year, the cleaners who came down under the horns.

## What moves them

[The Carrier](the-carrier.md). While the Three-Tone plays and the boards say "boarding", the dead walk to where their timetable puts them: the platform edge, the turnstile queue, a bench, the foot of the escalator. Anything between them and the platform edge is an obstruction to be cleared. When ZERO is between departures, they stand down and wait for a train, facing the track, behind the yellow line. That is why the night has rounds.

Their eyes glow signal amber because the Carrier is physically a lamp-proving current.

## The eight kinds

The Rapid's night rosters and Lodestar's cleanup logs, read together, describe eight kinds. The Zero Kings who were in the siding at 23:47 were out of earshot of the public address and are not among the dead.

1. **The Commuter.** Grey suit, beige trench coat, red tie. An inauguration guest.
2. **The Conductor.** Navy uniform, cap and ticket punch. Crew of the 23:47. One of them carries the [Dead-Man's Handle](the-conductor-of-047.md).
3. **The Zero King.** Paint-crusted HRT paint-shop overalls, a respirator pushed up on the forehead, a crown stencilled on the back. One of the Rapid's sacked livery painters; these are the few who were up on the concourse that night, and who are not counted with the staff.
4. **The Track Worker.** Orange vest and hard hat. Night staff.
5. **The Office Commuter.** Shoulder pads and trainers. An inauguration guest.
6. **The Token Clerk.** Cardigan and a glasses chain. Night staff.
7. **The Club Kid.** Neon sweatshirt and leg warmers. An Orbit party guest without skates.
8. **The Lodestar Cleaner.** A yellow suit torn open, a compass-star badge. Sent in from the cleanup of 1985, and noticeably harder to put down.

The forty skaters of the Party Car stand apart from the eight kinds as [the Rollers](orbit-rink.md).

## Habits

They commute. A dead man with nowhere to walk goes to the nearest waiting place and faces the track. Between rounds, stragglers are found waiting for a train. A Lodestar cleaner who is killed vents a pale cloud from the torn suit, which hides what is inside.

## The missing

Three of the 58 night staff have posters on the concourse: Dennis Malone, token clerk; Ruth Prescott, signalwoman; and Walter Hughes, track walker. The Rapid printed the posters on 2 January 1985. Nobody has asked about them since.

## In the map

The eight kinds are the ordinary zombies, drawn by district: Lodestar cleaners from round 8, mostly in the Cordon, the works and the Underneath; commuters and clerks in the concourse; track workers in the works; Zero Kings in the Boneyard. The Conductor appears once per match. They are not infected; they walk to where the Carrier sends them.
