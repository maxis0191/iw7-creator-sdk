# The Conductor of 047

**The Conductor of 047** is the train guard of the 23:47, Conductor **Edmund Pask**, forty-nine, of Eastbank. He carries the **Dead-Man's Handle**, the brass controller key of the cab, which he was to hand to the Chairman at midnight.

## The agreement

The Zero Line was sold as a railway with no drivers, and ZERO needed none. The Rapid's union accepted that on one condition, written into the 1982 working agreement: every train carries a guard, and the guard carries the key. The key was therefore ceremonial. It is a controller handle that controls nothing, and it was to be carried to the cab on a cushion and handed to Chairman Dellinger at midnight, so that the first driverless train in the country could be seen to be started by a man. The guard rides in the rear of the train, in car 6, and walks forward through the cars to the cab.

Pask was chosen for the inaugural run by seniority. Colleagues describe a careful man who polished the handle on his sleeve before every shift and said "mind the doors" before he said good morning.

## The crew

The crew of run 047 numbered four, counted on the manifest among the 312 people aboard (308 guests and the crew of 4). Pask's was the only name entered against the key.

## The Handle

A brass key about the length of a hand, with a red knob. It fits the controller in the cab of car 1 and nothing else. The plate beside the keyhole reads "DEAD-MAN'S HANDLE REQUIRED", which was a courtesy to the union and a prop for the photograph. Nothing was ever held down, and nobody was ever to hold it in car 6 or anywhere else. Without the key the cab stays dead; with it, whoever turns it is, for the length of the photograph, the driver.

## Appearance and behaviour

Navy uniform, brass buttons, cap with the Rapid's disc, a ticket punch on a cord. He carries a hand lamp that swings amber with every step and announces himself with a guard's whistle. He walks and never runs, and he closes the car doors behind him as he goes, one car after another, as a guard does. He is sturdier than the dead who surround him, and slower, as if there were somewhere he were not quite ready to go. When he reaches a locked cab he turns round and goes back, and the whistle sounds again.

## In the map

He steps out of the Party Car within 20 seconds of the road being set in the Signal Cabin, and walks through the train and along the platform toward the cab, pulling each car's doors shut behind him (they open again when he dies). He has three times the health of a zombie of the round, walks and never runs. Killing him drops the Handle, which shows as a key icon for the carrier and the team. If he is not killed he leaves with the round and returns at the start of the next, and he cannot be lost. Fitting the Handle in the cab takes 1.5 seconds. The Conductor is one of the eight kinds of the dead (M2).
