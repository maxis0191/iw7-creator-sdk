# The Voice of the Rapid (Evelyn Marsh)

**The Voice of the Rapid** is the recorded announcer of the Zero Line: a warm, exact, slightly amused female voice, recorded in 1984 by the actress **Evelyn Marsh**. It is heard on the public address horns of every station on the line, after the [Three-Tone](the-carrier.md), and it is the only voice that [ZERO](zero.md) possesses.

## Evelyn Marsh

Thirty-one in 1984, Marsh was a stage actress who had worked her way into radio commercials and, through a casting call at Lodestar's studio on Calder Road, into the contract of her life. Over three days, 14 to 16 November 1984, she read 4,112 items: numbers, station names, times, apologies, instructions, courtesies and the word "please" in eleven registers. The producer asked for "reassuring but not sleepy". She gave him a voice that sounded as though it had just heard something funny and decided not to say so.

She was paid a flat fee and credited nowhere. Her name survives on the studio session log, and on a tape box that says "MARSH · MASTER · DO NOT ERASE". Her whereabouts since 31 December 1984 are not recorded.

## How ZERO uses her

ZERO does not play her recordings; it assembles sentences from them, word by word and syllable by syllable, a process the engineers called "concatenation" and the cleaners called "stitching". Most announcements are built from words she said. Some are not. A sentence that Marsh never recorded can still be spoken in her voice, and the seams are not easy to hear.

## Eight announcements

1. "The 23:47 Zero Line service is now boarding at Platform A. Please stand clear of the doors." *(recorded)*
2. "We apologise for the delay. Your train will be with you shortly." *(recorded)*
3. "This is a Zero Line service to the Terminus. All stations to the Terminus." *(assembled from recorded words)*
4. "Watch the edge between the train and the platform." *(recorded)*
5. "Passenger, your train is waiting. Please return to your carriage." *(a name inserted: never recorded)*
6. "Midnight has been delayed. We regret that this is not unusual." *(never recorded)*
7. "The time is twenty-three fifty-nine." *(assembled from recorded words; the only time the Voice ever reads at the Terminus, and it reads it again and again)*
8. "Welcome back." *(never recorded)*

## The booth

An announcer's booth on the east wall of the Fare Hall looks down on the concourse. Its door is locked and an "ON AIR" lamp has been lit in it since the Midnight Service. A chair, a microphone and a cup of coffee are visible through it. The coffee is hot. Who is sitting in the booth, if anyone is, is not known.

## In the map

The Voice speaks the map's first full sentence when power comes on, announces the Roller rounds as a party-train announcement, and is the source of the Fare Hall pay-phone line. At the Terminus it reads out the time, calmly, and the time is always the same. It never announces an arrival. The locked Announcer's Booth with its lit sign is a secret in the Fare Hall.
