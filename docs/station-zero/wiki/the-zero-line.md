# The Zero Line

The **Zero Line** is the fifth line of Halcyon Rapid Transit and the first metro line in the country run with no drivers. It was inaugurated on 31 December 1984 and has had one service since: run 047.

## The line

Six stations on a route of about eleven kilometres: **Halcyon Plaza** (Station 0), **Calder Road**, **Marlow Street**, **Eastbank**, **Tidewater** and **Harbour Exchange**. Trains are routed by a dispatch computer, [ZERO](zero.md), which reads their position from a control tone, the [Carrier](the-carrier.md), carried in the running rails and the public address lines. The roundel is a black "0" on signal amber. Fares were sixty cents a token from the opening, with a rise of ten cents announced for 1 January 1985 as "the cost of the future".

## The bypass

The line's express service was to leave Platform A and reach Harbour Exchange without touching the older stations. The cheapest route for that bypass ran through an arch that the 1931 surveyors had marked "old sewer" and nobody had opened. Behind it lay the 1893 trunk main of the [Atmospheric](the-atmospheric.md), a bore for a parcel post. The bypass was not driven through it, and a six-car train never ran inside it. It was wound around the outside of the main, close beside it, like one pipe beside another, and following the tube's line saved eleven months of tunnelling. [Dr. Imogen Strand](dr-imogen-strand.md) wrote in October 1983 that the tube "answers" the Carrier through the wall; Lodestar filed her memo and kept the route.

## Run numbers

A run on the Zero Line is numbered by line and minute: line 0, departing at minute 47. Run 047 is therefore both a number and a timetable entry, which is how it came to be called "the 23:47". After the Midnight Service ZERO went on issuing the number to dead lamps for a year. On the film night the boards light again, and the machine numbers what it issues from DEPARTURE 1.

## The inauguration

The line was scheduled to open at the stroke of the new year with a loop from Platform A, as an express, so that its first passengers might return at midnight and see the year turn on the platform. See [the incident](the-incident.md) for what happened to the plan.

## Failure of the claim

The Rapid had promised the public a line that runs itself, and it does. It runs exactly one train, to a timetable it cannot close.

## In the map

The Zero Line is on every sign: the black "0" on amber on the Platform A roundels, the headsign "047 ZERO LINE", and the board in the Fare Hall that opens the night with "DEPARTURE 1 · ZERO LINE · BOARDING". When the players fit the Dead-Man's Handle in the cab, the headsign flaps to "047 TERMINUS". The line has no other trains in the map.
