# The Incident (the Midnight Service)

The **Midnight Service** is the name Halcyon Rapid Transit staff give to the departure of run 047 from Platform A at 23:47 on 31 December 1984. The papers called it the **Halcyon Plaza gas incident**. Nobody who was aboard has been recorded leaving the station since.

## The evening

The Zero Line, the first metro line in the country run without drivers, was inaugurated that night. The gala opened at 23:30 on the concourse of Halcyon Plaza: the Rapid's staff brass band, a banquet table, a ribbon across the fare gates, and 312 people aboard, 308 guests with a printed invitation and a crew of 4. Chairman Royce Dellinger cut the ribbon at 23:41 with a pair of brass scissors and said the Rapid had built "a railway that is never late". Boarding took five minutes. The doors of the six cars closed at 23:46.

## The minute

Run 047, "the 23:47", left Platform A at 23:47:00, due back at 00:00 after a thirteen-minute express circuit by way of the bypass that winds beside the 1893 dispatch main under the station. The train never ran inside the main; it ran in the bypass beside it. It entered the bypass. The control signal of ZERO, the Carrier, leaked through the wall into the tuned main, and the main answered. The train reached the place that ZERO had scheduled and no map shows, the Terminus, and stood again at its platform in the same second.

Every clock in the station stopped at 23:47:00. Standby generator G-2 was locked out in the same minute, and the log entry is unsigned and carries a tag reading "DO NOT RESTART". The passengers were dead, standing, and listening to the public address, which was announcing the next departure. The Three-Tone had sounded on every horn in the station, and it reaches everyone in earshot of the public address: the night staff on the concourse died of it where they stood, as the passengers did. The painters in the dead-end siding, out of earshot, did not.

## The record

The night roster has one entry for the next eight minutes: "23:55 Control informed", in the hand of the duty station supervisor, G. Halloran. None of the 58 night staff signed out. At 00:07 on 1 January 1985 the officer on the plaza detail reported "smell of gas", as a Lodestar liaison had instructed him five minutes earlier. By the afternoon the gas report was the official account. The entrances were plugged with concrete on 3 January, the quarantine canopy was up by the ninth, and Lodestar Signal & Telephone began its cleanup in shifts.

The cleanup sent about 140 workers down over five months. The last shift is logged on 21 June 1985. Nobody signed out of it either.

## What it was not

It was not a gas leak, and no gas has ever been found. It is not an infection: nothing spreads by bite, and the dead do not hunger. They are late, and a machine keeps telling them that their train is boarding.

## In the map

The Midnight Service is the film's whole backstory: the player starts one year later, in the Cordon, on the night of 31 December 1985. Every stopped clock, the "DO NOT RESTART" tag on G-2 in the Converter Hall, the night roster in the Staff Stair and the report in the Transit Police Post carry a piece of it. Restarting G-2 is the players' first act of making the station run again.
