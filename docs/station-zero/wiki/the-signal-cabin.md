# The Signal Cabin

**The Signal Cabin** stands at the east end of Platform A, up a steel stair above the platform, with a long window that looks west down the whole length of the deck. It is where the road was set for the Midnight Service, and it is the only place from which a signaller can see the entire platform at once.

## History

Built in 1931 with the station, the cabin housed a lever frame of forty painted steel levers that set the points and signals for the platform lines. By 1984 the frame was obsolete and had been replaced in function by ZERO, but it was kept, as a station tradition and as a backup, and eight of its levers were maintained and polished for the inauguration. A grey Lodestar control desk was shoved in beside the old frame to take over the work. Its single screen shows one line of text.

## What is inside

- **The lever frame.** Forty levers in colour bands, of which eight have polished handles and still move. Each polished lever has a brass plate naming a route: BYPASS UP, CROSSOVER, ZERO, and others.
- **The track diagram.** An illuminated panel of the Zero Line's approach, with lamps for each section.
- **A stove and a kettle**, as in any cabin, and a mug on a shelf.
- **The train register**, a ledger of every movement, open at 23:44. The last entry is three lever names, the three the diagram lights, in Signalwoman Ruth Prescott's hand.

Ruth Prescott was the night signaller. She is one of the three staff on the missing posters.

## The road

The road for run 047 was set from this frame at 23:44 by Prescott, on ZERO's instruction, three levers pulled by hand for the first and last time, and the frame was returned to normal once the train had passed. The east signal, at the end of the platform, dropped to red behind the train. It has remained red.

## The view

From the long window the whole platform is visible: the six silver cars, the stopped clock, the cess beside the east tunnel mouth. The room is cold and clean and very quiet. Prescott's mug is half full.

## In the map

The Signal Cabin is a small overwatch zone on Platform A East, up a steel stair at -160, with one stair in and one door out to the Converter Hall (D12). It holds the Deadeye Dewdrops machine, a marksman rifle wall weapon at 1500, and the lever frame, the second step of the road to Pack-a-Punch: three of the eight working levers are named on the lit diagram at random each match, and the players pull those three. A wrong lever springs them all back and drops four zombies from the ceiling vent. The RED Live Rail fuse waits in the relay cupboard after the road is set, and a hot-stick is hidden behind the frame in one of its three places.
