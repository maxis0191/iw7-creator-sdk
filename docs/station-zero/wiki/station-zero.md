# Station Zero

**Halcyon Plaza, Station 0**, called "Zero", is the central station of Halcyon Rapid Transit in Port Halcyon and the origin point of its measured network. A brass stud set in the floor of Platform A in 1931 marks chainage zero: every distance on the network, in every direction, is measured from it. The Zero Line was named for the stud before it was named for anything else.

The station is a vertical cut through ninety years of one city block. Each layer was built by people who did not know, or pretended not to know, what lay beneath them.

## The layers

**The street (1985).** The plaza above is sunken, ringed by concrete barrier slabs and roofed by the quarantine canopy: the [Cordon](the-cordon.md). The lit entrance canopy reading "STATION 0" is the only part of the station that still faces the city.

**The concourse (opened 1931, rebuilt 1978).** The Fare Hall, with its token booths, turnstiles and the great split-flap board, and the [Underpass Arcade](the-underpass-arcade.md) of 1978. It is dressed for the inauguration: bunting, a ribbon, a banquet table with fallen chairs. The concourse also holds [Shelter 7](shelter-7.md) and the [Transit Police post](transit-police-post-0.md).

**The platforms (1931).** Platform A, where run 047 stands with its doors open; the track pit; the [Signal Cabin](the-signal-cabin.md); and the dead-end siding the Zero Kings call the Boneyard.

**The works (1931 to 1984).** The [Car Barn](the-car-barn-and-the-sump.md), the Converter Hall with its [rectifiers](the-mercury-arc-rectifiers-and-g2.md), the Lodestar Exchange where [ZERO](zero.md) lives, and the Sump, the pump room and storm drain.

**Underneath (1893).** Ambrose Vayle's waiting salon, the Parlour, and the Bellows Vault, broken into by Lodestar's excavation in August 1983 and wired into the Zero Line. See [the Atmospheric](the-atmospheric.md).

**Nowhere.** The [Terminus](the-terminus.md), reached only from the cab of the 23:47.

## Character

Late 1984 and 1985: sodium light, cigarette machines, pay phones, tokens, cassette decks, green phosphor screens, relay logic beside the first microprocessors, roller discos in their last year of fashion. Cold War leftovers sit one door from a Victorian salon. The signage uses a clean transit grotesque for the Rapid, a compass-star serif for Lodestar and hand-painted gilt for the Atmospheric.

The station has one train and a great many clocks that agree with each other. It once served four lines, and their platforms lie behind the concrete plugs of the Cordon; the one that can still be reached is Platform A. A Lodestar cleaner wrote in an early shift log that it felt less abandoned than interrupted.

## In the map

The map is Station Zero: eighteen playable zones in five districts, plus the Terminus. The Cordon is the spawn. The concourse zones are the Fare Hall, the Escalator Hall, the Underpass Arcade, Shelter 7, the Transit Police Post and the Staff Stair. Platform A is split into a West and an East zone, with the Signal Cabin beside it. The works are the Car Barn, Converter Hall, Lodestar Exchange and Sump. Underneath are the Breach, the Parlour, the Bellows Vault and the Boneyard. Doors are bought with points; there are seventeen.
