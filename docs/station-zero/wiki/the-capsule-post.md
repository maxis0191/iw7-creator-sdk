# The Capsule Post

**The Capsule Post** ("the Post") is the network of pneumatic tubes under Halcyon Plaza: the narrow dispatch bores of the Atmospheric, opened out and refitted by Lodestar in 1983 and 1984 so that a brass capsule with one rider in it can be sent from one worksite to another. Nothing in it is a train, and nobody who built it would have called it transport. It is post. The rider is a parcel, and the plates on the hatches say so.

## History

[Ambrose Vayle's](ambrose-vayle.md) dispatch desk in the Parlour sent messages through four narrow tubes to counting houses around the plaza. When Lodestar's excavation reached the Parlour in 1983, its works engineers found the bores intact and noticed that they ran under the places Lodestar needed to connect: the street, the computer rooms, the depot and the Parlour itself. Rather than lay new conduit, they bored the old pipes out to take a larger cylinder. Parts went through first. Then, because a capsule that can carry a spanner can carry the man who owns it, a capsule with a seat.

The Zero Kings had been in the bores already. Undercoat's murals draw the network on the walls, from memory, as it was before Lodestar opened out a single pipe.

## The hatches and the ring

Four hatches stand at the ends of the Post: **PLAZA** (inside a Lodestar decontamination tent in the Cordon, a yellow steel frame over a Victorian cast-iron mouth), **EXCHANGE** (the end wall of a relay aisle, between two racks), **BARN** (a brick pier beside an inspection pit in the Car Barn) and **PARLOUR** (Vayle's walnut dispatch desk, the only untouched Victorian hatch, with gilt lettering that reads "DISPATCH"; capsule No. 12 stands on its own cradle in the arch beside it and is not part of the Post). Behind each hatch lie two capsules.

The bores run as a ring and one blower drives them all. A parcel sent in the direction the blower is pushing reaches a neighbouring hatch quickly. A parcel sent the other way has to go round.

## The address dial

Above every hatch is a brass ring with three enamel name plates, one for each of the other hatches, and a pointer. The rider turns the pointer to the address, drops the postage in the slot and is sent. Each plate carries a one, two or three lamp mark for the distance. Postage is 250, 400 or 550, and the ride takes five, nine or thirteen seconds. A hatch that has not been opened shows "TUBE SEALED" on its plate and cannot be addressed.

## The direction lever

In the Bellows Vault a lever sets the blower to **BLOW** or **DRAW**. Throwing it swaps which addresses are near and which are far. Nothing is ever out of reach: every hatch can be addressed from every other, in both settings, and the lever only changes what the trip costs. The organ in the Vault sounds the Departure Note while the tubes change over, and every hatch lamp turns amber until they have.

## The capsule

A copper cylinder with a glass canopy, a buttoned red leather pad from 1893 and a lap belt added in 1984. A parcel label holder on the lid flips to the name of the address the rider chose. The rider sits, the hatch seals with a thump of cork and leather, and the Bellows draws a breath. Through the glass the rider sees, as a blur, parts of the station that nobody walks: above the platform, behind the relay racks, under the Parlour's floor.

## What the hatch plates say

Lodestar's stencil on every hatch reads "INSPECTION CAPSULE · ONE PERSON · KEEP ARMS IN", with "POSTAGE" over the coin slot. Anything in the way of the hatch when a capsule leaves is drawn in with it and comes out of the far hatch behind the rider.

## In the map

The Capsule Post is the map's fast travel. It needs power. Each hatch is unsealed once for 750 points by holding Use on its pressure wheel. A rider dials any unsealed hatch and pays 250, 400 or 550 points for a ride of 5, 9 or 13 seconds, during which the rider cannot shoot and takes no damage. Each hatch has two capsules, and each returns 30 seconds after it leaves. Zombies within 48 units of the hatch at launch are drawn in and thrown out of the far hatch, knocked down for a second. The lever is in the Bellows Vault. Hatches near a living Night Inspector show "INSPECTION" and sell nothing. A rider who has just arrived cannot be sent again for 45 seconds (the dial shows "REST"). A marked player keeps the Night Inspector's mark through a ride. Bon Voyages makes the postage half price.
