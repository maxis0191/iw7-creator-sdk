# The Clocks

The station keeps **seven clocks** on ZERO's register. Six stand stopped at 23:47. The seventh, in the Terminus, runs, and stops one second short of midnight. All seven are the same business: thirteen minutes that nobody has taken.

## The stopped six

Every clock in the station stopped at 23:47:00 on 31 December 1984, the second that run 047 left Platform A. They did not run down or lose power; they stopped in the same second, to the same hand, whatever their make. The hands of each stand at 11:47, or 23:47, which on a twelve-hour dial are the same.

| Clock | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Street clock | the Cordon | on a lamp standard, face cracked by weather |
| The board clock | above the split-flap board, Fare Hall | the station's master clock face |
| The stud clock | hanging over the zero stud, Platform A West | the network's reference clock |
| The foreman's clock | the glass office, Car Barn | a wall clock in an oak case |
| The Exchange clock | the Lodestar Exchange | a plain electric dial on the end wall |
| Vayle's long-case clock | the Parlour | stopped at 11:47 since 1897 |

Vayle's clock is the oldest and the oddest. The Atmospheric's attendant wound it every morning; on 2 November 1897 it read 11:47 and has not moved since. Lodestar's electricians tried to start it in 1983 and failed. On a twelve-hour dial it keeps the same time as the other five, so that ZERO's register lists it as one of its own.

## The seventh

The seventh clock hangs on the wall of the Terminus platform. It runs, alone, and at the right pace, from 23:59:19. At 23:59:59 it stops, and its visitors go home. It has never been seen to show midnight.

## The thirteen minutes

The Midnight Service was due back at 00:00, thirteen minutes after it left. The station's clocks are therefore thirteen minutes short of midnight, and [ZERO](zero.md), which counts only arrivals, believes that the train has not yet come in. Wind a clock thirteen minutes forward to 12:00 and it strikes: that clock, at least, has had its midnight. Four of the clocks keep a bell for the purpose, and each bell is different. When all four have struck, ZERO takes it that 047 is due and opens boarding. It does not log an arrival: expecting the train is as far as the winding goes.

## The stud

Brass, flush with the platform floor, the zero stud under the stud clock fixes the origin of the network. If it is struck with a blade, every clock in the station ticks once, in every room at the same instant. Nobody has said why. Nobody has said what the three clocks beyond the four do, either.

## Why they stopped

[The Carrier](the-carrier.md) and the tuned main beside the bypass met at 23:47:00. The clocks keep the time of the train that left. By the clocks it never left, and midnight never came.

## In the map

Seven clocks. Four are quest clocks, in the Fare Hall, Platform A West, the Car Barn and the Parlour: winding each takes a 3 second hold on the key, the hands sweep to 12:00, the clock strikes, the Three-Tone plays and a burst of zombies rises in that zone. Four wound clocks are the first step of the road to Pack-a-Punch. The Cordon and Exchange clocks are stopped and take no part in it, and the Terminus clock is the only one that runs. Knifing the zero stud makes every clock tick once.
