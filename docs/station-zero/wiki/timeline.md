# Timeline

Dates are as given in the records the archive holds.

**The Atmospheric**

1. 3 March 1851: Ambrose Vayle born in Tanner's Row, Port Halcyon.
2. 12 March 1889: Vayle patents "a dispatch tube tuned to its load" (notebook page 1).
3. 1890: the Vayle Pneumatic Syndicate begins the tube. On 3 October he notes the clay "keeps a sound"; on 21 November he orders the glazed brick.
4. 7 April 1892: the dispatch desk is tested; a note reaches the harbour before it is sealed.
5. 2 January 1893: the Halcyon Atmospheric Dispatch Company is incorporated.
6. 14 May 1893: the post opens: a 290-metre trunk main and a ring of dispatch tubes, twopence a parcel.
7. 1 November 1897: Vayle's last notebook entry, in the Bellows Vault; seen there that evening. The season of "passenger dispatch", in twelve padded capsules, is in its last day.
8. 2 November 1897: the Silent Dispatch. Eleven capsules come home at noon with their riders dead and smiling; Vayle's own, No. 12, comes home empty; the long-case clock reads 11:47.
9. 20 December 1897: the Company is wound up and the trunk main bricked.

**The station**

10. 1927: Halcyon Rapid Transit is formed.
11. 2 March 1931: surveyors set the zero stud in Platform A.
12. 9 March 1931: Halcyon Plaza station opens; the bricked arch is noted as "old sewer".
13. 1952: standby generator G-2 installed in the Converter Hall.
14. 14 September 1961: Shelter 7 locked, stocked for 90 people for fourteen days.
15. 11 November 1978: the Underpass Arcade opens after the concourse refit.
16. 1979: Royce Dellinger becomes chairman; the fiscal crisis begins; the Rapid's paint shop is closed and its livery painters are sacked; Orbit Rink opens on Marlow Street.
17. Winter 1980: the sacked painters settle in the dead-end siding and go on painting. They are the Zero Kings.
18. February 1982: the Kings find the arch at the end of the siding broken from the inside.

**The Zero Line**

19. March 1982: Lodestar wins the contract for driverless operation, with its "no failed runs" clause.
20. 14 April 1983: Strand's first tape: the bypass through the "old sewer".
21. 17 August 1983: the excavation breaks into the Parlour; Strand's second tape.
22. 12 October 1983: the test trolley; Strand's memo; Pruitt files it. Third tape.
23. 20 February 1984: Strand records that the tube answers the Carrier (tape 4).
24. March to August 1984: six cars fitted in the Car Barn.
25. 9 August 1984: tape 5; the inaugural run published.
26. 1 November 1984: ZERO begins shadow running.
27. 14 to 16 November 1984: Evelyn Marsh records the Voice of the Rapid.
28. 14 December 1984: Strand reads the log specification (tape 6).
29. 29 December 1984: Strand asks Dellinger to postpone; he refuses (tape 7).

**The Midnight Service**

30. 31 December 1984, 23:30: the gala opens on the concourse.
31. 23:41: the chairman cuts the ribbon.
32. 23:44: Signalwoman Prescott sets the road. 23:46: the doors close; Strand's last tape.
33. 23:47:00: run 047 departs. The clocks stop. G-2 is locked out. The Three-Tone sounds on the public address, and everyone in earshot of it dies.
34. 23:55: "Control informed." No one signs out.
35. 1 January 1985, 00:07: "smell of gas" is reported.
36. 3 January 1985: the entrances are plugged. By 9 January the Cordon stands.
37. January 1985: Lodestar's crews go down in shifts. In the siding, the Zero King called Undercoat begins to paint warnings: all five murals are his work of 1985.
38. 21 June 1985: the last shift is logged. No one signs out.

**After**

39. 31 December 1985: the film night. The boards light: DEPARTURE 1.
40. 7 March 1986: STATION ZERO opens in cinemas and closes eleven days later.

## In the map

The map is set at entry 39. The earlier entries live in the signage: the dates on the Platform A West kiosk papers, Strand's tapes and Vayle's pages, the night roster and the stopped clocks.
