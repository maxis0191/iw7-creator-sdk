# The Underpass Arcade

**The Underpass Arcade** is the shopping underpass of Halcyon Plaza, opened on 11 November 1978 as part of the concourse refit: a long, low passage of brown and orange tile, a ceiling of lit panels (half dead), and five bays along its north side: four shops and a photo booth. It was meant to turn commuters into customers. For six years it did.

## The shops

**Needle Drop.** A record shop run by Gil Amory, who stocked what the Rapid's night shift wanted at two in the morning: dance singles, cassettes, and a wall of sleeves. Inside stands a **jukebox**, installed in 1983 so that customers might sample the stock. A coin buys a change of tune for the whole passage.

**Copy Stop.** A copy and print shop. Its photocopiers still flash and cycle under their lids through the night, lighting the passage in white and green, a blueprint machine hums in the back, and a passport-photo corner stands by the door with its stool turned to the wall. Nobody has collected the last job in the tray; it is the same page, printed forty times.

**Hole Number Nine.** A doughnut and coffee counter, the mate of the Rapid's lunchtime. Its owner, Dolores Kemp, served Dr. Imogen Strand the same order every working day for six years. On the last day Strand left a cassette tape behind the till, with a note asking her to keep it safe.

**Heel & Key.** A shoe repair and key-cutting booth, shutter half up, with a row of repaired shoes waiting on a shelf that the owners never came back for. The sign says "WHILE YOU WAIT".

**The photo booth.** A corner booth with a curtain and a bench, taking four frames for a fee. It was installed in 1978, and the sample strip on its side shows the same grin four times, the fourth slightly wrong.

## The floor

A floor polisher, left running by a cleaner on the night of the Midnight Service, circles in the middle of the passage. A sale card in hand-lettered capitals hangs in the window of every shop: "NEW YEAR REDUCTIONS". Neon lights each shopfront in its own colour: red, green, blue, white, a yellow that has gone orange.

## The photo booth strip

A strip from the booth, paid for in coin, has four frames. In the first three the sitter is alone. In the fourth someone is standing behind them. It is thought that this is a fault in the machine, and equally thought that it is not.

## In the map

The Arcade is a long straight for runners with five pockets to duck into. It holds the Double Takes machine at the photo booth, a mystery wheel position inside Copy Stop, sub-machine gun and marksman rifle wall weapons, the jukebox in Needle Drop (100 points: changes the zone's music), the photo booth secret (500 points: a strip of four frames, the fourth showing someone behind you), a Zero Kings tag pointing toward the Boneyard and Strand's second tape in Hole Number Nine. The blast door to Shelter 7 is at its west end.
