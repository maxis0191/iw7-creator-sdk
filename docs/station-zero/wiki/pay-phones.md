# Pay Phones

**Pay phones** were installed throughout Halcyon Plaza during the 1978 refit, as part of the Rapid's effort to appear modern and, more usefully, to stop passengers using the station's staff telephones. Three still work, each in a steel hood with a coin slot and a laminated list of "numbers of use to travellers". They were serviced by Lodestar, whose telephone division maintained the exchange behind them.

## The line

Lodestar's engineers ran the Zero Line's control tone, the [Carrier](the-carrier.md), along spare pairs of the telephone cabling as well as the rails and the public address lines, in order that ZERO could reach any lamp, loudspeaker or handset in the station. The pay phones were therefore connected to the machine. After the Midnight Service the lines were dead to the city but not to the station.

## The three phones

Each of the three has its own habit, and none of them rings for the same reason.

| Place | What it does |
|---|---|
| The Fare Hall, by the token booths | Rings once in a night, in the same two-tone pattern, loud enough to be heard in the next room. If it is answered, the [Voice of the Rapid](the-voice-of-the-rapid.md) speaks: a line assembled by ZERO from [Evelyn Marsh's](the-voice-of-the-rapid.md) recordings, in the manner of a courtesy announcement, beginning "This is a courtesy call. The 23:47 is now boarding." Then the line goes dead. |
| The Transit Police Post, at the public counter | Rings when the Night Inspector arrives. If it is answered, a flat voice reads out his duty roster, line by line, in ZERO's fixed capitals. The last line is the Inspector's own. |
| The Car Barn, by the foreman's office | Silent on its hook. If the handset is lifted, it rings in the ear: a number nobody dialled is tapped out, and an engaged tone answers. The cleaners' logs say the number is [Dr. Strand's](dr-imogen-strand.md). The handset is always warm. |

Some of the Fare Hall's sentences were never recorded; the seams between words are audible if one listens for them.

## Collect calls

Cleaners' logs call the effect "collect calls": the station telephoning those inside it, at its own expense. The logs also record that nobody has found a way to ring out, although a handful of cleaners tried.

## Directory

The laminated list on each hood gives the usual numbers (taxis, the Rapid's lost property office, the Transit Police) and a last line, in the Rapid's grotesque, that reads: "FOR TRAVEL INFORMATION, DIAL 0." Dialling 0 in the old station connects to nothing. The phones ring on their own account.

## In the map

A pay phone stands in each of three zones: the Fare Hall, the Transit Police Post and the Car Barn, and each has its own event. The Fare Hall's rings once per match and plays a line assembled from the Voice. The Police Post's rings when the Night Inspector arrives and reads out his roster. The Car Barn's is silent unless it is lifted, and then dials Dr. Strand's number, which is engaged. It is one of the map's secrets.
