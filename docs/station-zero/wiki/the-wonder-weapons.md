# The Wonder Weapons

Four unusual tools turn up in the station, and each was made by someone for a reason that had nothing to do with the dead. None is a bigger gun. Each is a different verb: **pay**, **place**, **send** and **paint**.

## EXACT CHANGE

**Made by** a sacked paint-shop hand who was tired of paying. He took one of the Rapid's green enamel fare boxes and rebuilt it into a carbine: a brass hopper on top, a coin chute for a barrel, a chromed turnstile arm for a lever. It fires transit tokens, a fare plate riveted on its side reads "FARE 30", and the glass hopper shows the tokens dropping one at a time.

**Why.** He had been paying a fare to ride a train he lived under. He decided the fare box could pay him back.

*What it does:* every shot is bought with the shooter's own points, and a one-token head-shot kill on the first target pays the base fare of 30 back, at most once every second and a half.

## LIVE RAIL

**Made by** the Rapid's third-rail gang, who needed a way to prove a rail dead without touching it: a collector shoe from under a train, bolted to a yellow insulated hot-stick, fed by a cable drum worn on the hip. The gang's foreman added a stake launcher in 1981, so that a test lead could be pinned to a wall rail from a safe distance, and a fuse holder whose fuses are named for signal aspects: **RED**, **AMBER**, **GREEN**.

**Why.** Nobody in the gang wanted to be the last man to learn a rail was live. It was never meant to be pointed at anything that moved.

*What it does:* two stakes planted in the air hold a standing arc between them. Whatever crosses it is stopped and pushed back, and the fuse in the holder decides what else the arc does.

## THE DISPATCHER

**Made by** Ambrose Vayle in 1893 as a hand pistol for addressing parcels: brass, bellows and oxblood leather, an ivory grip, and where a barrel would be a round rubber address stamp in a brass collar, wet with violet ink. Its plate reads "HALCYON ATMOSPHERIC DISPATCH Co. · No. 12". Every parcel on the Atmospheric was stamped with its destination and the tube did the rest. The pistol still works. It does not ask what the parcel is.

**Why.** For the days when the desk's four hatches were too slow, and the parcel was too far from the desk.

*What it does:* a shot stamps an address on a zombie, and a pull of the bellows sends every stamped zombie to the place the shooter is looking at. A send uses up its stamps, and the bellows needs three seconds to refill before it can send again. Its upgraded form is **FORWARDING ADDRESS**: more stamps at once, a harder send, and a sent zombie that survives walks back toward the address it was stamped for before it remembers the shooter.

## TOP TO BOTTOM

**Made by** Undercoat, the last of the Zero Kings: a dented steel pressure pot on a shoulder strap, a brass spray lance and three sockets for cans, covered in the crowns of the paint shop. The Kings were the authority's own livery painters, and knew the colour code of the railway better than its managers. Undercoat found out in 1985 that the Carrier reads colour as a signal: paint a dead man the right colour and he does what the colour says. "Top to bottom" is what a painter calls a car painted from roof to sill.

**Why.** To say something to the dead in the only language they still obey.

*What it does:* the colour tells the dead what to do. **MAGENTA** is "ALL STAND": the dead walk to the nearest waiting place and stand behind the yellow line. **CYAN** is "WET PAINT": a slick on the floor. **YELLOW** is "HAZARD": a marked zombie bursts when a player's weapon kills it, hitting the six nearest.

## In the map

Two come from the mystery wheel: EXACT CHANGE, and TOP TO BOTTOM with its MAGENTA can (CYAN is in the Boneyard stash, YELLOW drops from the first Lodestar Cleaner that the player has painted). LIVE RAIL is built at the Car Barn workbench from three parts, each in one of three places per match, with fuses found in the Signal Cabin (RED, once the road is set) and the Converter Hall (GREEN, once all four Capsule Post hatches are open). THE DISPATCHER is earned in the Parlour's quest "Undelivered": arrive at three different hatches, bring the message cylinders to the dispatch desk, and charge the pistol at the organ in the Bellows Vault.
