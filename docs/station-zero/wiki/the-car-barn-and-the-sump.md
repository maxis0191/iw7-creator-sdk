# The Car Barn and the Sump

The **Car Barn** and **the Sump** are the two southern rooms of the station's works: the depot where the Zero Line's cars were fitted out, and the pump room below it that keeps the station dry. They share a drain and a grate, and a good deal of oil.

## The Car Barn

The biggest room of the station, entered from the platform's east tunnel mouth by a roller door at the end of the cess. Its ceiling is high enough for an overhead crane on rails, from which a wheel set still hangs. A spare car stands on four jacks with its bogies rolled out. Two inspection pits are cut in the floor with their work lamps still on. A wheel lathe, a paint store, a wall of tools on shadow boards, and oil, oil, oil.

The foreman's glass office stands against one wall. Foreman Stan Brodie kept his accounts there in a ledger no one else could read, and a wall clock in an oak case that he wound each Friday. It stopped at 23:47:00 with the others. A **workbench** by the lathe carries a vice, a drawer of fuses and a hand-lettered sign: "NOBODY BORROWS THE 14 MM".

Six cars were fitted here between March and August 1984, each with a Carrier receiver and a lamp-proving lamp in every door. The paint store holds the pots the cars were never painted with; a manager had cancelled the livery to save money, and the train went out silver. Foreman Brodie noted on the wall chart in November, "Told them they'd regret it." It is still pinned there.

## The Sump

Reached by the pit grate in the Car Barn, the Sump lies about three metres lower: a pump room with ankle-deep black water, a slow current, three big pumps (one running and shaking), a steel catwalk round the walls and tide marks higher than the water. Things float: party hats, a clipboard, a single roller skate. A storm culvert opens in one wall, its grille bent outward and a rope hanging from it, the way in used by anyone who has come in from the street without a ticket.

The Zero Kings painted a mural inside the mouth of the culvert. The one pump that runs has not stopped since the Midnight Service, and the water in the Sump has stayed at the same mark for a year.

## Why they are together

The works were laid out in 1931 so that anything dropped in the depot would roll toward the drain. A pit grate and a catwalk door connect the Barn to the Sump.

## In the map

The Car Barn is the biggest room on the map, a loop round the jacked car with pits to jump or avoid, ceiling 448. It holds the Quickies machine, a mystery wheel position, light machine gun and rifle wall weapons, the Live Rail workbench, quest clock 3, the BARN Capsule Post hatch, a pay phone, a skate-wheel trophy and Strand's fifth tape. The Sump (D15, 1500 points) is a pocket with a catwalk ring, water that slows players slightly, a heavy weapon at 2000, a mystery wheel position, a skate-wheel trophy and mural 2.
