# Chairman Royce Dellinger

**Royce Dellinger** (born 1923) was chairman of Halcyon Rapid Transit from 1979, the year of the fiscal crisis, to the night of the Zero Line's inauguration. He cut the ribbon at 23:41 on 31 December 1984 and rode car 1 of the 23:47.

## Career

The son of a tram driver, Dellinger began as a conductor on the old Harbour trams, took evening courses in accounting, and rose through the Rapid's finance office on a reputation for balancing books that could not be balanced. As chairman he cut the night shifts, closed three ticket offices and sold two depots, and was cheerfully frank about each. His public line was that the Rapid would be saved by one bold stroke.

## "A railway that is never late"

The stroke was the Zero Line. Dellinger's speeches in 1983 and 1984 returned to the same promise: a railway that is never late, because a computer cannot oversleep, quarrel or strike. It was printed on the invitation, painted on the gala banner in the concourse, and repeated by the chairman to the press in the Fare Hall in the minutes before the ribbon. He pronounced it as though it were a family motto.

## The warning

[Dr. Imogen Strand](dr-imogen-strand.md) asked him on 29 December 1984 to postpone the opening. By her tape, he answered that a railway that is never late cannot start late. He had by then invited three hundred and twelve guests, and the press, and the mayor's office, and he had promised the Rapid's creditors a midnight photograph.

## The ribbon and the bottle

At the gala he cut the ribbon across the fare gates with brass scissors, shook the hand of the Lodestar vice-president, and went through the gates to car 1. A bottle of champagne was left on the coupler of the cab end, to be broken on the return. He had a glass of the same wine in his hand. The ribbon was cut at 23:41, and its two halves still hang from the gates. The unbroken bottle sits where it was left.

## What is recorded

The manifest lists him in car 1, the directors' saloon, behind the cab. The record does not say more than that.

## In the map

Dellinger never appears. A ribbon hangs across the fare gates in the Fare Hall, with the banquet table and the brass band's music stands; the champagne bottle is unbroken on the coupler of car 1 on Platform A East.
