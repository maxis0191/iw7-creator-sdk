# The Cordon

**The Cordon** is the quarantine that the city of Port Halcyon raised over Halcyon Plaza in January 1985, after the Midnight Service: concrete plugs in the station entrances, concrete barrier slabs round the plaza, a torn canopy over everything, and two Lodestar decontamination tents in the middle. It has been manned by the Rapid's Transit Police ever since.

## Construction

The entrances were plugged on 3 January 1985 with poured concrete, and by 9 January the plaza was walled. The plaza itself is sunken, so the Cordon sits in a bowl: barriers on the rim, rain collecting in the middle, a canopy of heavy plastic sheeting on steel poles, torn by one winter's weather. The barrier slabs are stencilled "CORDON · HRT". Sodium floodlights on stands light them. A trailer generator, parked inside, feeds the lamps.

The station's own entrance canopy, **"STATION 0"**, still stands over the plugged stair and, wired to Lodestar's trailer, still glows. Lodestar was granted the central ground for its cleanup, and from January to June 1985 its crews worked from here, going down in shifts and up in fewer.

## The tents

Two Lodestar decontamination tents stand in the middle of the plaza. The first is a staging tent and holds the **PLAZA hoist**, a yellow Lodestar steel frame over a Victorian cast-iron mouth cut through the pavement, used to send tool kits and, later, the crews down by capsule. The second contains a spray corridor in which each worker was washed with foam before going home. Few of the last crews were washed. Anything that wades in the foam moves slowly and moves its arms slowly too.

## Left behind

A Channel 9 news van, parked at the barrier on the last night, with its mast up and its door open. A stopped street clock reading 23:47. Candles and flowers at the barrier, replaced each week for a year by a woman nobody sees. And on a hoarding by the entrance, the film's poster: "STATION ZERO. Next stop: nowhere."

## The police detail

A pair of Transit Police officers stood each shift at the barrier. The officer who reported "smell of gas" on the night of the Midnight Service has stood here since. He has been told he is on a standing detail. He has not been told what it is a detail against.

## In the map

The Cordon is the spawn zone, a sunken plaza with a canopy at +620 and the lit entrance "STATION 0". It has long sightlines and two exits, the entrance stair into the Fare Hall (door D1) and Lodestar's hoist stair to the Transit Police Post (D2). It holds the Up 'N Atoms machine, a pistol-class and a carbine wall weapon, the Decon Foam trap in the second tent (zombies in the foam are slowed and attack at half rate), the PLAZA Capsule Post hatch, the Channel 9 van with Strand's first tape, and a street clock that is not a quest clock. Spawners come from under the barrier slabs, the storm grating, the tents and the canopy's service hatch.
