# BLEND-LAYERS: what a stock layered surface is made of, and how to write one (worker BL1, 28 Nov 2026)

Investigation only: files and DXBC, no game, no build, no bake, no Forge request. Everything below was read from bytes on this PC.
Tags: **[m]** measured from bytes by a script (in `C:\studio-data\sz\film\blend\`), **[r]** read in code or docs, **[a]** assumed or not yet proven.

## 0. The three answers

| | answer | tag |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | The layer stream is **per layered surface, `stride` bytes a vertex, `stride` = 8 to 56 by technique (not a constant)**; 28 B for `*622n_89_91_282n`, 36 B for `*387n_86n_354n_362n`, 12 B for `*622n_274n`; the whole cp_disco zone holds 2,194,900 B for 818,917 vertices (2.68 B a vertex on average, because plain surfaces have none). It does **not** hold blend weights: it holds the per-layer **UV** (float32 pairs, a world-space planar projection) and, per layer, a **2 x 2 UV rotation** packed as unorm8x4. The weights are the **vertex colour G, B, A** (slot 0). | [m] bytes + 254/254 techsets + 422/422 chunks |
| Q2 | The layered lit technique reads **both**: COLOR0 (weights, G/B/A only; R is not read by the pixel shader) from slot 0 **and** TEXCOORD3, 4 (, 7), 5, 6, 9 (, 10, 11, 13), COLOR1 from slot 1 (the layer stream). The 2-layer techniques (51 of 254 techsets) do not read COLOR0 at all. Prepass, velocity and the unlit vertex-colour techniques read neither stream. | [m] DXBC input signature + disassembly |
| Q3 | **Yes.** For a stock `*` material the world writer must emit per vertex: colour `R=255, G,B,A = our weights` (layers 1, 2, 3) and a layer record of the technique's stride (uv floats from our planar projection, rotation bytes `FE 7F 7E FE`-style for the surface's axis). Weights are free (a chosen paint, e.g. dirt at the wall foot). The file of a `*` material is `_<rest>.json` (ZoneTool `clean_name`, `*` to `_`), also for its state and constantbuffer files. | [m] shader + bytes; "free weights in the running game" is [a] until the proof of section 5 |

## 1. Q1: the layer stream, from bytes

### 1.1 Where the bytes are
`C:\studio-data\store\cache\cp_disco\assets\transient_zones\cp_disco.gfxmap_tr` (38,777,784 B, parsed with the SDK's `worldkit/iw7_dump.py parse_transient_zone`, script `q1_zone.py`) [m]:
`vertexCount` 818,917 (44 B each), `vertexLayerDataSize` **2,194,900**, `cellCount` 2. The same file exists in the store cache for cp_final, cp_frontend, cp_rave, cp_town, cp_zmb and mp_breakneck. The surface table is `...\assets\maps\cp\cp_disco.d3dbsp.gfxmap` (11,606 surfaces, `q1_surf.py`; `GfxSurface.tris` = `vertexLayerData u32, firstVertex u32, maxEdgeLength f32, vertexCount u16, triCount u16, baseIndex u32`, `iw7_dump.py:193`). Nothing was missing: no "stop and report" case.

### 1.2 How a surface addresses its stream [m] + [r]
Studio's renderer reproduces the engine's world draws at 99.91 % exact against a RenderDoc capture (`C:\studio\view\renderer.md:159`; `v2/build.py:47-49`): `baseVertex = 0`, slot 0 buffer offset = `firstVertex * 44`, slot 1 buffer offset = `surface.vertexLayerData` (bytes), indices are relative to `firstVertex`. So the layer record of vertex `firstVertex + i` is at `vertexLayerData + i * stride`: **one record per vertex of the surface's vertex range, aligned with slot 0**.
Measured on cp_disco: surfaces sharing `(vertexLayerData, firstVertex, vertexCount)` form a chunk (569 chunks, 489 distinct offsets); the 3,836 surfaces at offset 332,428 are plain surfaces pointing at a shared tiny region (offset unread when the layout has no slot 1). For a layered chunk, `stride = (next distinct offset - offset) / vertexCount`; 422 chunks with only `*` materials (all strides measured, `q1_stride.py`, `q4_cost.py`): strides 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40, 44, 48, 52, 56, always a multiple of 4; vertex-weighted mean **17.57 B**, median 16 B. Offsets are 4-byte aligned. Stock layered surface `flags` / `sortKey` are not special (same distribution as plain: flags 0/1/8/9, sortKey 0).

### 1.3 What the stride is a function of
The stride is a function of the **lit technique's vertex shader inputs** (Q2), not of the surface and not of the layer count alone. Exact over all 597 techsets of cp_disco's 1,528 `*` materials (`layer_layout.py`, `layer_layouts_all.json`): 

```
bytes per vertex = 4 * comps(TEXCOORD3) + 4 * comps(TEXCOORD4) + 8 [if TEXCOORD7]
                 + 4 * (number of present TEXCOORD5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 13 and COLOR1)
```
(comps = components used in the VS input signature mask). 254 of 254 techsets with a measured single-material chunk fit; 422 of 422 all-star chunks have the predicted stride (0 bad). Elements sit in this order, floats first: `TEXCOORD3, TEXCOORD4, TEXCOORD7, TEXCOORD5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 13, COLOR1`; the float elements were finite and bounded in 254/254 chunks (`q2_order.py`); the order of the unorm elements among themselves is [a] (decl order), proven only on the two decoded techsets.

### 1.4 What the bytes hold [m]
Decoded rows of the three requested surfaces (`q1_bytes.py`, `q1_387.py`, `q1_uvfit.py`):

**`*622n_89_91_282n`** (surface 337/338, offset 197,992, firstVertex 24,239, 45 vertices, stride 28; base `w/concrete_bare_patches_clean_03`, layers `w/ceiling_waterstains1`, `w/asphalt_flooring_stain_01`, `w/water_puddle_footprints_02`; techset `w_l_sm_replace_r0c0s0o0nl0ql0p0_m1c1_m2c2_b3cx3s3ol3nbl3v3pa3`): record = `float2 uv1, float2 uv2, float2 uv3` (24 B, TEXCOORD3 = uv1+uv2, TEXCOORD4 = uv3), then `u8x4` (TEXCOORD5) = `7E 00 FE 7E`. Row 0: uv = (-1.1133, 2.0625) three times, tail `7E 00 FE 7E`. The three uv are equal here.
- uv is a **world-space planar projection**: fitted per surface as `uv = A * pos + c`, 103 of 383 single-plane surfaces fit to 1e-2 (the others are multi-plane chunks); this surface: `u = -y/128 + 21.186`, `v = -x/128 + 2.34375` (vertex (36, 2854.5, 286) gives (-1.1133, 2.0625); (184.29, 2753.35, 286) gives (-0.3231, 0.9040), both exact). Coefficients are 1/128 times small integers (1, 2) with axes swapped and signed by the face's dominant axis.
- the `u8x4` decodes (VS: `byte/127 - 1`, `vs_793b.txt`) to (-0.008, -1, 0.99, -0.008) = a **2 x 2 rotation `[[0,-1],[1,0]]`** (90 degrees: u = -y, v = -x). The pixel shader uses it as `dp2(normal.xy, v8.xy)`, `dp2(normal.xy, v8.zw)`: it rotates the layer's normal map into the surface frame (`ps_d633.txt` lines 150-151).

**`*387n_86n_354n_362n`** (7 surfaces, offset 1,176, firstVertex 1,292, 479 vertices, stride 36; base `w/concrete_wall_brown_02_wet_less` + `concrete_stain_01_dec_wet`, `ls_conc_cracks_01_dec_nodsp`, `t7_decal_grunge_garbage_spread_01`): `float2 uv1, float2 uv2, float2 uv3` (24 B), then three `u8x4` (TEXCOORD5, 6, 9) = `FE 7F 7E FE` x 3 (decoded (0.99, 0, 0, 0.99): identity rotation). Layers do not share one mapping here: uv2 equals uv1 in 30 % of the vertices, has the same length in 144 and twice the length in 28 of the vertices with a visible uv1 (row 0: (0.4842, 0.0449), (0.9685, 0.0898), (0.9685, 0.0898)), the rest differ by other offsets or scales; so the per-layer tiling and offset are written in the stream, per surface, and are not recoverable from the material.

**`*622n_274n`** (offset 65,928, 1,050 vertices, stride 12; one layer): `float2 uv1` (TEXCOORD3 mask xy) + `u8x4`.

### 1.5 The weights, and the correlation with the wear
The pixel shader of `*622n_89_91_282n`'s lit technique (`d633cc91b66f90d2`) declares `COLOR0` used **`.yzw`** (G, B, A) and nothing of R; the VS passes COLOR0 straight through (`mov o1.xyzw, v1.xyzw`). In the PS: `layer1 weight = alpha(layer1 texture at uv1) * COLOR0.g` (`ps_d633.txt:109-111`), `layer2 weight = alpha(uv2) * COLOR0.b` (`:115-117`), `layer3` uses `1 - COLOR0.a` as the reveal threshold against its height map (`:121-136`). Same for `*387n`'s PS `a20d7cfc9e95046b` (`v1.y`, `v1.z`, `v1.w`; `ps_a20d.txt:72,111,131,142,201`). Across the 254 lit pixel shaders: COLOR0 used `.yzw` in 105, `.yz` in 96, `.xyz` in 1, `.xyzw` in 1, **absent in 51** (all the 2-layer techsets) (R2's observation "R 255 constant" is therefore not a weight).
Vertex colours of the two surfaces (`q1_387.py`): `*622n_89_91_282n`: R 255 on every vertex; G 0..255 mean 149.5, B mean 140.2, A mean 39.7; `*387n_86n_354n_362n`: G/B/A means 184/177/132, 29 % fully white, 84/115/192 vertices at exactly 0 in G/B/A (layer absent) and 275/280/218 at exactly 255. So wear is in the colour (0 = layer off), the stream is geometry. **Not done: a visual correlation against a frame** (no game): the claim "G, B, A are layers 1, 2, 3's weights" rests on the shader code, not on a picture.

## 2. Q2: what the layered techniques read

The chain, for `*622n_89_91_282n` [m]:
`materials/_622n_89_91_282n.json` (`techniqueSet->name` `w_l_sm_replace_r0c0s0o0nl0ql0p0_m1c1_m2c2_b3cx3s3ol3nbl3v3pa3`, `subMaterials` 4) -> `techsets/<techset>.techset(.txt)` (27 technique slots; slots 13 to 26 are the lit pass, one technique) -> `techsets/lmap_specenv_g0[0]_ng0[0]_c0_r0_ql0_n0_nl0_s0_om0_p0_g1[1]_c...tech.technique` -> strings `793b6c27a0da5736` (vertex shader), vertexdecl `pp.fcc0.ft0t0.fnn.ftt2.ft1t3.ft2t4.3x0t5.f`, `d633cc91b66f90d2` (pixel shader). Dump root: `C:\Users\ezvalo\Documents\IW7-Runtime\iwz-stage2\kitimport\ztwork\dump\{techsets_cp_disco\techsets, cp_disco\materials}` (`vs\*.cso`, `ps\*.cso` are DXBC).
Vertex shader input signature (ISGN, `q2_isgn.py`; D3D disassembly by `d3ddis.py` through `d3dcompiler_47`):

| input | mask | meaning (from the shader and the bytes) | slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| SV_POSITION0 (v0.xyz) | xyz | position | 0 |
| COLOR0 | xyzw | **weights G, B, A** (R unused by the PS) | 0 |
| TEXCOORD0 | xyzw | base uv + lightmap uv | 0 |
| NORMAL0, TEXCOORD2 | | normal, tangent | 0 |
| **TEXCOORD3** | xyzw | layer 1 uv (xy) + layer 2 uv (zw), float32 | **1** |
| **TEXCOORD4** | xy | layer 3 uv, float32 | **1** |
| **TEXCOORD5** | xyzw | layer rotation, unorm8x4, `byte/127 - 1` in the VS, output COLOR1 | **1** |

`*387n`'s lit technique (VS `95e71121f2cf22d7`, PS `a20d7cfc9e95046b`, decl `...ft2t4.3x0t5.fx1t6.fx2t9.f`) adds TEXCOORD6 and TEXCOORD9 (two more rotations). The techniques that do not read the stream: `position_only`, `vertcol_simple_zpp_*` (decl `pp.f`/`pp.7`), `velocity_*`, `vertcol_untextured_*` (`pp.fcc0.f`), `vertcol_shaded_*` (`pp.fcc0.ft0t0.3`, colour only) (techset listing of `*387n`'s techset, `q2` session). So **only the lit pass binds slot 1**, which is why a layered surface whose stream is zero draws the base only and no error shows: with zero uv and zero rotation the layer textures sample one texel and the normal rotation collapses (this is the state our compiler writes today, 16 zero bytes a vertex, `iwzmap.cpp:2427`).
Vertex-declaration grammar (`pp.fcc0...`): not decoded further; the element sizes are given by the VS input signature, as fitted above. Engine-side layout (the D3D input layout object) is not in any dump; Studio's capture-fitted renderer is the only witness of slot 1 [r].

## 3. Q3: our own surfaces on a stock layered material

1. **Material file names [m]**: ZoneTool `clean_name` (`x64-zt-weapons` `iw7/assets/material.cpp:43-60`) maps `*` to `_`; on disk: `dump/cp_disco/materials/_387n_86n_354n_362n.json`, `techsets/techsets_cp_disco/techsets/state/<techset>/_387n_86n_354n_362n.{statebits,statebitsmap,stateinfo}`, `.../constantbuffer/<techset>/_387n_....{cbi,cbt}`. SDK `kit_import.py:290,292,297,299` builds all four paths from the raw name with `*` (impossible on Windows): the fix is `fname = mat.replace('*', '_')` in those paths only; the asset reference keeps `*387n...`. The same bug family is at `sz2_kitzone.py:49-52` and `fx/fx_import.py:128-132`.
2. **The material is map-local [r]** (R2 section 2): the same `*` name in another map is another material. Before a `*` material of cp_disco joins a build whose template is cp_zmb, check the name against the template zone's material csv (collision test in step 2).
3. **Per vertex, what our writer must emit**: colour `R=255, G=w1, B=w2, A=w3` (0 = layer off; the four-layer techsets read only these three channels; 5- and 6-layer techsets still use `.yzw`, the extra layers are driven by other means, [a]); and a layer record of the material's technique stride built as: `uv_k = (pos . U) * s_k + c`, `V` likewise (planar projection on the face's dominant axis, 1/128 per unit like stock, `s_k` = the per-layer tiling we choose: stock uses 1 and 2), rotation bytes = the 2 x 2 matrix `(m00, m01, m10, m11)` of the axis swap (the VS decodes `m = byte/127 - 1`, so encode `byte = round((m + 1) * 127)`: `+1 -> 254`, `0 -> 127`, `-1 -> 0`; stock writes 126 or 127 for 0, 253 or 254 for +1). Pure rule, no data from the dumps except the layout table.
4. **Weights are free** because they are plain vertex colour in slot 0; painting dirt at a wall foot = `G = 255 * smoothstep(h1, h0, z - floor)` per vertex, which needs vertices at the foot (one extra edge loop, section 4.3). [a] for the in-game look until section 5.
5. **One stock layered material on new geometry**: the surface only needs a `GfxSurface` with the material and the offset; the compiler already copies flags / sortKey from a template surface of the same material, else the first lit-opaque template surface (`iwzmap.cpp:2105-2122, 2175-2186`), and stock layered flags are not special (1.2).

## 4. Q4: the implementation plan

### 4.1 world.bin version 2
Header `0x575A5749, 2`. Sections as in version 1 (`mapgen.py:90-128`, `iwzmap.cpp:517-541`: materials, 44 B vertices, u16 indices, surfaces) with these changes:
- vertex records unchanged; **colour is now written, not 255** (the compiler copies it unchanged, `iwzmap.cpp:531`, no compiler change for colour);
- surface record `<5I6f` (44 B, `iwzmap.cpp:244-253` `src_surface`) becomes `<5I6fII` (52 B): `+ layer_offset u32` (bytes, relative to the start of the layer blob, 4-aligned), `+ layer_stride u32` (0 = no layer record);
- after the surfaces and before the 6 bounds floats: `u32 layer_size`, then `layer_size` bytes (the blob; record `j` of a surface = vertex `first_vertex + j`, `vertex_count * stride` bytes).
Version 1 stays readable (stride 0 everywhere).

### 4.3 Cost on the one document
- **Triangles**: 0 added by the layers themselves (they are per pixel). A foot or gradient paint needs vertices where the weight changes: one extra edge loop per painted wall face (+2 triangles per wall segment, +~2 vertices per loop corner), a few dozen triangles for a hall, and the painted vertices no longer weld with the clean ones (+5 to 10 % vertices on those surfaces, [a]).
- **Bytes**: `stride` B a layered vertex in the zone's `vertexLayerData` (and in `world.bin`): stock vertex-weighted mean **17.6 B** (`q4_cost.py`: 61,261 layered vertices, 1,076,220 B), 28 B for `*622n_89_91_282n`, 36 B for `*387n`. On the film base (`C:\studio-data\sz\film\base_lit\world.bin`: 27,608 vertices, 27,608 triangles, 94 materials): if every vertex were layered 474 KiB at the mean (755 KiB at 28 B, 971 KiB at 36 B), which is the upper bound; its concrete / tile / plaster surfaces (`concrete_wall_painted_01` 796, `concrete_floor_polished_01` 708, `dt_tile_ceramic_white` 560, `iwz_tile_charcoal` 520, `clk_concrete_bunker_dark` 412, `concrete_wall_04_seam_grey` 388, `plaster_molding_02_white_trim` 448, `concrete_trim_floor_01` 136: 3,968 vertices) are the realistic first set: 70 KiB at the mean, 111 KiB at 28 B. The control room (19,694 layered vertices of 119,365): 338 KiB at the mean. The stock zone is 2.19 MB of layer bytes: nowhere near a limit [a: no engine limit on `vertexLayerDataSize` is known].
- **Pixels**: a layered pixel shader samples 2 to 5 more textures (stock cost, not measured here).

## 5. The in-game proof (one frame pair, after step 9)
Document: a test wall 512 x 271 and a floor in `*622n_89_91_282n`, painted as a 3 x 3 grid of quads (channel G, B, A each at weights 0, 128, 255), a second wall in `*387n_86n_354n_362n`, lit by the base lights; one camera pose facing them; `forge`/`studio live` run per the brief of that task. Frames: (A) all weights 0, (B) the grid painted, (C) the stock instance of the same material in `stockref` at a comparable pose for the look. Must change: in (B) against (A), per quad, the mean absolute difference of the linear luminance rises with the weight in the channel's layer (monotone over 0 < 128 < 255 for G and B), and stays below 0.5/255 outside the painted quads (measured with `control_compare.py` regions / `r2` metric style, per-quad boxes from a projected corner list). Fail cases and what each means: no change at all = slot 1 not bound (check the surface offset and the stride in the compiler log, section 4.2 step 3); change but wrong tiling = uv rule wrong (the stock fit of section 1.4); black or garbage = stride mismatch (the engine reads past the surface's block). The `*` name rename of step 2 is proven by the same build.

## 6. What is established, what is not
