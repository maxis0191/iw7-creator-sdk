# Transit Police Post 0

**Transit Police Post 0** is the station house of the Halcyon Rapid Transit police at Station Zero, off the east end of the concourse. It was the force's busiest post, and the post of the officer who signed the report that said "gas".

## The force

The Rapid's Transit Police are a uniformed force of about 300, funded by the authority, answerable to the chairman and, through him, to nobody. Their shoulder patches read "HRT TRANSIT POLICE · PORT HALCYON". During the fiscal crisis they were paid late and then paid in instalments, and the force learned to run on goodwill and the smell of coffee. Post 0, numbered for the station, was the oldest and the most staffed.

## The post

Entered from the Fare Hall by a public counter with a glass screen, from the Cordon by a stair that Lodestar cut through the pavement, or from the staff stair behind. Inside:

- **The duty desk,** with the incident report still in the typewriter. It reads, in part: "00:07, 1 Jan 1985. Smell of gas reported on plaza. Plaza cleared. Source not found. No further action." It is signed with the initials of the officer on the plaza detail.
- **The holding cell,** its door open, its bench scratched, and its inner wall scratched with something harder than a nail.
- **The armoury cage** and **the evidence cage,** side by side, behind one counter. In the evidence cage is a micro-cassette tape with a buff tag, "EVIDENCE 84/1127 · HAZARD · IN ADVANCE", handed in at the counter on 30 December 1984 by a woman from Lodestar. It was never opened.
- **Lockers,** a locker room with a dark end, and a wall map of the network. One station on it has been blacked out with a marker.

## The report

On the night of the Midnight Service the duty sergeant, Joyce Albright, told her officers to report what Lodestar's liaison told them to report. The officer on the plaza detail was a patrolman who had been on the force two years. He typed the report himself, in his own words, one-fingered, at 00:19, and signed it. He has not forgotten that he did.

## In the map

The Transit Police Post is a fortress with three ways in: the street, the hall and the staff stair. It holds the Bang Bangs machine inside the armoury cage, a rifle at 1500 and a shotgun at 1000 in the cage, the evidence cage as one of three possible places for the Live Rail hot-stick, Strand's third tape on an evidence tag, a pay phone that rings when the Night Inspector arrives, and the typed report. The doors are the public counter door (D5, 1000), the Cordon stair (D2, 750) and the staff stair's cage door (D17).
