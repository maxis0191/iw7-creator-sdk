# Halcyon Rapid Transit

**Halcyon Rapid Transit** (HRT, "the Rapid") is the municipal transit authority of Port Halcyon, formed in 1927 by merging two tramway companies. In 1984 it ran four underground lines and 41 stations, and in the year of the Zero Line it was close to insolvent and enormously proud.

## The disc

The Rapid's logo is a white disc with a black horizontal bar that does not reach the edges. Staff call it **the held breath**. It appears on enamel signs, on coveralls, on the brass tokens and, rather larger than anyone intended, on the first pages of Lodestar's contract. Its colours are white, black, and a worn enamel green used on fare boxes and bench slats.

## Station Zero and the stud

HRT's central station, Halcyon Plaza (Station 0), opened on 9 March 1931. Surveyors had set the network's **zero stud**, a brass stud in Platform A, a week earlier. Every distance on the network is measured from it, which is why the station is called Zero and why the newest line took the number.

## People and roles

- **Chairman Royce Dellinger** chaired the authority from 1979 and promised "a railway that is never late".
- **Chief Fare Inspector Horace Pell** headed ticket inspection; his thirty-one years of service made him the oldest member of staff.
- **The Transit Police**, a separate uniformed force funded by the Rapid, patrolled every station from a numbered post. Post 0 is at Station Zero.
- **Night staff**, 58 at Station Zero by the end of 1984 after the cuts, ran everything between last service and first.

## Decline and the bet

Between 1979 and 1982 the authority cut its night staff by a third, closed its paint shop and sacked the painters, and let the maintenance of its oldest stations slide. The Zero Line was the authority's answer: run by a computer, built cheaply, opened with a gala, and paid for by savings the bypass was meant to deliver. Lodestar Signal & Telephone promised that the system would run itself. The Rapid took the promise at its word, which was the intention of the contract.

## Voice and style

HRT notices are set in a clean transit grotesque, in capitals, with times in the twenty-four hour form. Typical notice: "FARES ARE REVISED FROM 1 JANUARY 1985. THE RAPID REGRETS THE INCONVENIENCE." Its public tone is that of a long marriage: it apologises constantly and changes nothing.

## In the map

The Rapid is everywhere on the map as signage. Its disc appears on the Engineer's coverall, the token booths, the "HRT" barrier slabs of the Cordon, the Transit Police patches ("HRT TRANSIT POLICE · PORT HALCYON") and the green fare box that a Zero King rebuilt into a carbine. The night roster hangs in the Staff Stair, with its empty OUT column.
