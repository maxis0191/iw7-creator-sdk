# Dr. Imogen Strand

**Dr. Imogen Strand** (born 1946) was the lead engineer of Lodestar Signal & Telephone on the Zero Line. She found the Parlour in 1983, understood what it was, warned her employer in writing, and on the night of 31 December 1984 rode the 23:47 in the cab, "to keep a hand on it". She has been missing since the Midnight Service.

## Life

Born in Eastbank, the daughter of a ship's radio operator, she took a doctorate in network theory and joined Lodestar's exchange division in 1974. Colleagues recall a tall, patient woman who ate lunch at the same doughnut counter every day, drank tea from a Lodestar mug, and carried a grey pocket recorder everywhere. She talked to it as other engineers talk to their notebooks. It was a habit that began as a way of dictating test results and became, by 1983, a diary.

## The Parlour

When the excavation for the express bypass broke through on 17 August 1983, Strand was the first engineer to walk into the salon. She read [Ambrose Vayle's](ambrose-vayle.md) notebooks over the following weeks and set up a field desk at the foot of the cut, with a lamp that she was in the habit of leaving on. On 12 October 1983 she wrote the memo that Lodestar filed. She spent the next fourteen months finding a way to prevent the run that she was not permitted to prevent.

## The tapes

She recorded eight tape diaries between April 1983 and the minute of departure and left copies where she hoped someone would listen: a journalist's folder, a doughnut counter, a police evidence tag, a seat, a foreman's drawer, her own desk. The eighth ends in the cab. See [the tapes](strand-tapes.md).

## Her fate

The record does not say whether she is among the dead. Her eighth tape stops in the middle of a sentence, in the cab, and nothing else of hers has been found.

## Character

Dry, exact, and not given to alarm. Her recorded voice treats the end of the world as a scheduling problem. She is the only person in the record to have used the word "answer" about the tube, and she meant it as she meant everything: literally.

## In the map

Strand never appears, but her work does. Her field desk, with its lamp still on, stands in the Breach. Her eight tapes are the map's main lore pickups, in the Cordon, the Arcade, the Transit Police Post, Platform A East, the Car Barn, the Exchange (two) and the Terminus. The printer's manifest is in the Exchange. The question of her fate is left open on purpose.
