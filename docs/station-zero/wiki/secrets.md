# Secrets

The station keeps ten things for people who look, and a good deal that is simply left lying about. Nobody is told about any of them, and none needs the road to Pack-a-Punch. This page is a field guide, from the cleanup logs, the tapes and the night staff's own notes, with where each one is.

## The ten

**1. The eight tapes.** [Dr. Strand's](strand-tapes.md) eight micro-cassette diaries: in the Channel 9 van in the Cordon; in Hole Number Nine in the Arcade; on an evidence tag in the Transit Police Post; on a seat of car 2 on Platform A East; in the Car Barn; two in the Lodestar Exchange; and the last on bench 4 at the Terminus. Heard in order, they tell what happened.

**2. The six notebook pages.** [Vayle's](vayle-notebook.md) leaves, each where his work was found: the Fare Hall (framed as "station history"), Shelter 7 (on a poster), the Breach, the Parlour (two) and the Bellows Vault.

**3. The five murals.** The [Zero Kings'](the-zero-kings.md) paintings: Platform A West on the pit wall, the Sump inside the culvert mouth, the Bellows Vault, and the Boneyard (two). Standing in front of each for three seconds logs it. All five were painted by Undercoat in 1985, and together they are a map of the narrow tubes, drawn from memory by someone who had ridden them before Lodestar bored them out.

**4. The zero stud.** On Platform A West under the stopped clock. Struck with a knife, it makes every [clock](the-clocks.md) in the station tick once.

**5. Capsule No. 12.** Vayle's own capsule stands on its cradle in the west arch of the Parlour. Eleven were sent on the last day of the season; his is the twelfth, and the only one with a window on the inside. Its lid cannot be opened, and something has written the players' passenger numbers on the inside of the glass.

**6. The manifest.** The line printer in the Lodestar Exchange prints the current players' names on the passenger manifest, between lines of the timetable of the dead.

**7. The photo booth.** In the Underpass Arcade. Paying 500 produces a strip of four frames; in the fourth, someone stands behind you.

**8. The Announcer's Booth.** High on the east wall of the Fare Hall: a window, locked, with "ON AIR" lit. Inside are a chair, a microphone and a cup of coffee that steams.

**9. The pay phones.** Three, with three different events: the Fare Hall's rings once per match and plays a line assembled from the Voice; the Transit Police Post's rings when the Night Inspector arrives and reads out his roster; the Car Barn's rings only when it is lifted, and gives Dr. Strand's number, engaged. See [pay-phones.md](pay-phones.md).

**10. The song.** Three brass skate-wheel trophies from the Orbit party, hidden in Platform A East, the Car Barn and the Sump. Activating all three plays the map's own song.

## In the map

All ten are in the map from launch, and none needs the main quest. Counting everything that can be found and logged, the map holds 25 collectables: 8 tapes, 6 notebook pages, 5 murals, 3 other documents (the Inspector's ledger, the typed police report and the printer's manifest) and 3 trophies. The tapes show their text on the Tab screen. The Zero Kings' stash in the Boneyard, which holds the CYAN can for Top to Bottom, opens for a player who has looked at three of the five murals. The photo booth costs 500 points; the jukebox in Needle Drop, which is not a secret, costs 100.
