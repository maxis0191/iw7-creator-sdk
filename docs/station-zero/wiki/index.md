# The Zero Line Archive

**STATION ZERO** is a 1986 "transit horror" picture by Willard Wyler, which nobody remembers buying a ticket for. Its tagline is "Next stop: nowhere." It follows a Transit Cop, a Graffiti King, a Signal Engineer and a Night Desk Reporter into the sealed central station of Port Halcyon on the night of 31 December 1985, one year after the city's first driverless train left Platform A at 23:47 and, by every clock in the station, never left. The dead there are commuters: not hungry but late, and a machine keeps telling them that their train is boarding. This archive keeps the film's world as a record, the way a fan site would, and treats it as real. Each page closes with an "In the map" section about the playable level, giving zone names and what a player can do there.

## Open questions

The archive records these and does not answer them.

1. Is Dr. Strand dead? Her eighth tape ends in the middle of a sentence, in the cab. ([dr-imogen-strand.md](dr-imogen-strand.md), [strand-tapes.md](strand-tapes.md))
2. Who cut the eyes out of Vayle's portrait in the Parlour, and who rode capsule No. 12, the one that came home empty? ([ambrose-vayle.md](ambrose-vayle.md))
3. ZERO cannot log a failed run and has never logged an arrival. What happens if the run completes? ([zero.md](zero.md))
4. Who is in the Announcer's Booth, where "ON AIR" has been lit for a year? ([the-voice-of-the-rapid.md](the-voice-of-the-rapid.md))
5. What is the second platform printed beyond the fold at the Terminus, and whose train is waiting at it? ([the-terminus.md](the-terminus.md))
6. The Night Inspector punches tickets. Whose list is he working from? ([the-night-inspector.md](the-night-inspector.md))
7. Lodestar's last shift was sent to recover "the core". What is missing from the empty bay in ZERO's rack? ([zero.md](zero.md), [lodestar-signal-and-telephone.md](lodestar-signal-and-telephone.md))
8. Why does the zero stud make every clock tick, and what are the three clocks beyond the four for? ([the-clocks.md](the-clocks.md))
9. How did Undercoat paint four figures in the heroes' colours in 1985, months before the night of the film? ([the-zero-kings.md](the-zero-kings.md))
10. What fits the hole in the Zero token? ([tokens.md](tokens.md))
