# Run 047 (the 23:47)

**Run 047**, called **the 23:47**, is the inaugural train of the Zero Line: six silver cars, unpainted, with the headsign "047 ZERO LINE". It left Platform A at 23:47:00 on 31 December 1984, and by every clock in the station it never left. It stands at the platform with its doors open and its interior lights on. A bottle of champagne sits unbroken on the coupler of car 1: the christening was to take place on the return.

The manifest lists 312 people: 308 guests and a crew of 4. Their seats were assigned by invitation, which accounts for the way the cars divide.

## Car by car

**Car 1.** The cab and the directors' saloon. Chairman Royce Dellinger rode here. The driver's cab holds the controller, with a keyhole plate reading "DEAD-MAN'S HANDLE REQUIRED". The train is driverless and the controller is for show: the Rapid's union insisted on a key, a guard to carry it, and a seat in the cab, and the Chairman was to turn the key at midnight for the photograph. [Dr. Imogen Strand](dr-imogen-strand.md) rode in it, "to keep a hand on it".

**Car 2.** Council members and the press. A micro-cassette tape lies on one of the seats, left by Strand as she boarded.

**Car 3.** The Lodestar delegation: systems engineers in good suits who did not intend to be on the train and had been told to be.

**Car 4.** The first riders, forty guests drawn from a public lottery for the privilege of the inaugural minute.

**Car 5.** Rapid staff and their families.

**Car 6.** The **Party Car**, chartered by [Orbit Rink](orbit-rink.md) for its New Year "Roll-In". Streamers, a mirror ball, a sound system. Forty skaters in satin jackets boarded it; forty pairs of their street shoes stand in a neat row inside the door.

## The state of the train

Cars 1 to 4 stand at the platform; cars 5 and 6 stand inside the east tunnel mouth, beside a narrow walkway used by track staff. Every interior is intact and lit. No car has a mark of paint on it, which is the whole of its fame in some circles: the last unpainted train in the city.

The train does not move. It is, in ZERO's reckoning, at the platform at 23:47:00 and also due at midnight, and the two entries have not been reconciled. See [the incident](the-incident.md).

## In the map

Run 047 is the Platform A East zone. All six cars are walkable end to end. Cars 1 to 4 stand at the platform, cars 5 and 6 in the tunnel mouth. Car 6 is the main spawn of the Roller rounds, and a skate-wheel trophy is hidden on the train. Car 2 holds Strand's fourth tape. The cab is the fourth step of the road to Pack-a-Punch: the Dead-Man's Handle is fitted there and the train takes everyone in car 1 to the Terminus.
