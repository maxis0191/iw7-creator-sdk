# The Terminus

**The Terminus** is the place that run 047 reached on the Midnight Service, and the destination that [ZERO](zero.md) scheduled for it. It is Station Zero **as a timetable knows it**. ZERO never saw the station; it holds a printout of it, and the Terminus is that printout made into a place, at the size of a room. It is on no map of the network. It can be reached only from the cab of the 23:47.

## What it is made of

Paper. Every surface is green-bar fan-fold printer paper, of the kind that fills the printer room in the Lodestar Exchange, enlarged until a sheet is a room: pale green and cream bands across the floor, the walls and the ceiling, sprocket holes the size of dinner plates down the edges, perforations where the walls meet. Everything on it is printed in dot-matrix, a little out of register. The platform edge is a row of dashes, the yellow line is a band of asterisks, the columns are columns of times, and every time reads 23:59. The light is flat office light, the light over a desk, and nothing in it is dirty or scuffed because nothing in it was ever touched.

## What is there

Nothing that a timetable does not contain, and what there is has been counted:

- The platform edge, and the yellow line behind it.
- Signs in the Rapid's lettering, printed: "TERMINUS", "047", and "WAY OUT" with an arrow that points into a wall of times.
- Four benches, drawn in outline, solid enough to sit against, numbered 1 to 4.
- One clock.
- On the floor, in rows, **312 pairs of printed footprints**, each pair with a passenger number, all facing the track. Three hundred and twelve is the number of the guests and crew of the 23:47.
- A machine where a ticket validator would stand, under a sign: "FARE VALIDATION". It is the only solid, coloured object in the place, and it punches whatever is put into it, and punches it better.

There are no zombies, no flapping boards and no people.

## The clock

The clock is the seventh on ZERO's register and the only one that runs. It starts at 23:59:19 and runs for forty seconds. At 23:59:59 it stops. Midnight does not come, and the visit ends one second before it. The run has still never arrived.

## The footprints

On a visitor's first arrival, one more pair of footprints lies at the end of the last row, numbered 313 onward, in the colour of each visitor. The ink is wet. They are there on every later visit and have never been seen to dry. Nobody printed them.

## Sound

None of it is music. Under the quiet there is the Departure Note, very low. Somewhere a line printer runs and never stops. Now and then the [Voice of the Rapid](the-voice-of-the-rapid.md) reads the time, calmly, and it is always the same time.

## The second platform

Beyond the platform edge the paper continues, blank, to a fold. On the far side of the fold, printed very faintly, is the outline of a second platform with a train standing at it. Nobody has reached it, and nobody has said whose train it is or where it was going.

## The tape

[Dr. Strand's](dr-imogen-strand.md) eighth tape lies on bench 4. It was recorded in the cab of the 23:47 and ends in the middle of a sentence.

## The visit

Visitors arrive in an identical, spotless car that has never been scratched. The place keeps no one: when the clock stops, they are back in the car at Platform A, and its doors open. It is, so far as anyone can tell, what ZERO believes the night to be.

## In the map

The Terminus is the Pack-a-Punch room, a separate box far from the station, reached only by the cab of the 23:47 after the road to it is built: power, four clocks wound, the road set in the Signal Cabin, the Conductor killed for the Dead-Man's Handle, the Handle fitted. The visit lasts 40 seconds, and the team may not go again for 150 seconds. Downed players travel with the team, and while every living player is away the round's spawning pauses. The machine uses the stock interaction. Strand's eighth tape is the one pickup. No zombies spawn.
