# The Mercury-Arc Rectifiers and G-2

The **Converter Hall** of Halcyon Plaza converts the city's alternating current to the direct current that drives the Rapid's trains. It holds two instruments that matter: the mercury-arc rectifiers, the most beautiful light in the station, and the standby generator G-2, which was locked out at 23:47 on 31 December 1984 and has stood silent behind a tag since.

## The rectifiers

Two glass bulbs, each the size of a man, with many arms rising from a rounded base. In the base lies a pool of mercury, and when a rectifier is energised a violet arc dances above the pool, jumping from arm to arm and filling the hall with a light that flickers on every wall. They were supplied in 1930 by a firm that no longer exists, for the opening of the station, and sit in two wire cages with warning plates in the Rapid's early typeface. The glass is thin and the light is pure. Station electricians were forbidden to look at them for long and all did.

Between the cages stands a **marble switchboard** with knife switches and brass meters, whose needles are the same amber as the Zero Line roundel. After 1984 nobody switched anything on it by hand.

## G-2

**G-2** is a diesel standby generator, installed in 1952 beside a G-1 that was scrapped in 1976. It exists to keep the station's essential lamps and the signal system alive if the city's supply fails. It carries a brass plate and a bright red tag:

> DO NOT RESTART

The log entry that locks it out is timed 23:47 on 31 December 1984. It is unsigned. The tag is in Lodestar's lockout format, which Lodestar's contractors used on every set they did not want to run again. Its breaker is the station's power switch; the generator has not turned a revolution since.

## Why it matters

The rectifiers and G-2 are the station's heart, and both were stopped at the same minute as the clocks. To throw the breaker is to start the Zero Line's power again, and with it ZERO's lamps, the Capsule Post's pumps and the horns. Lodestar's cleanup crews found the tag and obeyed it for five months, in the belief that something at the station was waiting for a restored supply. They were right that it would do something with it.

## In the map

The Converter Hall holds G-2 behind a cage of rectifiers and the marble switchboard. Throwing the G-2 breaker is the power switch of the map: after 2.6 seconds of the diesel turning over, the light banks come on zone by zone outward from the hall and the rectifiers strike violet. The Flashover trap costs 750 points: the switchboard throws the hall dark and the rectifiers flash, and every zombie in the hall is blinded for 8 seconds, standing with its hands over its eyes. It has a 60 second cooldown. The hall also holds a shotgun wall weapon at 1250, one of the three possible places for the Live Rail cable drum, the GREEN fuse board and a fuse box. A jammed keypad door leads to the Exchange.
