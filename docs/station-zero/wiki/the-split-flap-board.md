# The Split-Flap Board

**The split-flap board** is the departure board in the Fare Hall of Halcyon Plaza and the only face that [ZERO](zero.md) has. Six metres wide, lit from behind, it flaps in banks, every flap a card printed with one letter or numeral, and it is the brightest object on the concourse. It was installed by Lodestar in the autumn of 1984, replacing a chalkboard.

## The board

Fourteen rows of forty cards, driven by relays, with an amber backlight behind the glass. The clatter of a full change can be heard on Platform A. Its typeface is the Rapid's transit grotesque, white on black, with the Zero Line's amber for the header row. A glass dial of the station's master clock sits above it, stopped.

## What it says

On the night of the Midnight Service it read "DEPARTURE · 047 · ZERO LINE · BOARDING". For a year afterwards the board was dark. On the film night it lights again, addressed to whoever is standing under it. ZERO uses it to state the next thing it wants. Typical readings:

- "DEPARTURE 1 · ZERO LINE · BOARDING". The first line, with a second beneath it: "POWER · G-2 OFFLINE", and a third with an arrow and "CONVERTER HALL".
- "047 · DUE 00:00 · NOW --:--". The run it is waiting for. The four dashes are filled one at a time as the clocks are wound, and when the last is in, the line reads "047 · DUE 00:00 · BOARDING". The board never says "ARRIVAL".
- "CLOCKS 2/4". A tally of what has been wound.
- "ROAD SET FOR 047". The signal at the east end of the platform has gone green.
- "047 · TERMINUS · BOARDING". The only entry on the board that names a station that is not on the map.

The board counts each reissue of the departure, so the number at the top climbs with the night: "DEPARTURE 12 · ZERO LINE · BOARDING". Between departures it shows "STAND CLEAR" and goes quiet.

## Reading it

Staff who knew the board report that it had no sense of urgency before the Midnight Service. Now it flaps constantly, the way a person clears their throat. A change of message is preceded by a full-bank whirr, in which every card turns at once, so that the hall is briefly filled with a sound like applause.

## Companion signs

The overhead signs of the concourse point the same way as the board's arrows, to the Converter Hall in the first instance. They were installed by the same contractor and follow the same typeface.

## In the map

The board hangs in the Fare Hall and shows the round as a departure; it is how the machine talks to the players. At the start it says "DEPARTURE 1", with "POWER · G-2 OFFLINE" and an arrow to the Converter Hall. After power it changes to "047 · DUE 00:00 · NOW --:--" and shows clock progress as "CLOCKS 0/4", then "047 · DUE 00:00 · BOARDING", "ROAD SET FOR 047", and "047 · TERMINUS · BOARDING" once the Handle is fitted.
