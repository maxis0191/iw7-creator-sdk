# Shelter 7

**Shelter 7** is a civil-defence fallout shelter built in 1961 off the concourse stairs of Halcyon Plaza, stocked for 90 people for fourteen days, locked, and forgotten by the authority that owned it. It was found again in 1985 by the Lodestar cleanup crews, who used it as a canteen.

## History

In 1961, with the city's civil-defence committee at its most anxious, forty-one shelters were designated across Port Halcyon. The planners found that the clay beneath Halcyon Plaza was of a density that muffles any concussion. On the poster that lined the shelter's corridor they quoted a Victorian engineer on the subject: "A bell struck below is not heard above" (A. Vayle, 1890). They had found his trunk main in the survey, and, being sensible men, they filed that sheet of the plan in a drawer.

The shelter was filled with cracker tins, water drums, bunks and a hand-cranked ventilator, and locked on 14 September 1961. A key was filed with the authority. The authority lost the filing cabinet in 1974.

## The cleaners' canteen

When Lodestar's crews went down in January 1985 they cut the blast door, found a larder untouched for twenty-four years, and made the shelter their mess. They ate the cracker tins, which were fine, and sat out their breaks on the bunks, which were damp. A half-eaten meal still sits on the nearest table beside a thermos. The crews' notice board lists the shifts. The last entry was for June.

## Appearance

Painted concrete, two rows of steel bunks, water drums, cracker tins stacked to the ceiling, a wall of yellowing instruction posters ("KNOW YOUR AIR", "SHELTER IS A DISCIPLINE") in a typeface nobody has used since. The blast door stands half open, a hand wheel on the inside. A steel supply locker, restocked by Lodestar's site security with clearance charges, stands against the end wall.

## Why it matters

Shelter 7 is the Rapid's accidental proof that the plaza was known to be strange. The civil-defence planners had the Atmospheric's trunk main on their survey, had read [Vayle's](ambrose-vayle.md) page, and had chosen to treat it as a feature of the ground.

## In the map

Shelter 7 is a pocket zone off the Underpass Arcade, reached by the half-open blast door (D7, 1250 points) and a ramp down to -96. It holds a mystery wheel position, a heavy-shotgun wall weapon at 1500, a cable drum (one of three places for that Live Rail part), a supply locker that gives one free lethal refill per player per round, and Vayle's second page on a 1961 poster. It has one door: the best loot per square metre and the worst place to be caught.
