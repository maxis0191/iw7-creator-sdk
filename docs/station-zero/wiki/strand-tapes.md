# Strand's Tapes

[Dr. Imogen Strand](dr-imogen-strand.md) dubbed her eight micro-cassette diaries and left copies where someone might find them. They are numbered in her order.

**Tape 1 · 14 April 1983**
"Tape one. Strand, Lodestar, Station Zero site office. The Zero Line opens on New Year's Eve next year with no drivers, and the express bypass is the problem. The cheapest route is an arch the 1931 surveyors marked old sewer, bricked and never opened. If it holds, we save eleven months. I have asked for the drawings. I am told asking is a formality."

**Tape 2 · 17 August 1983**
"Tape two. The cut broke through this morning. Behind it there is a salon: parquet, gas chandeliers, and a wall of tuning forks in a walnut frame. A company called the Halcyon Atmospheric ran a pneumatic parcel post here from 1893, and for one season in 1897 sent people through it in capsules. Its builder, Ambrose Vayle, left notebooks. He tuned the main like an organ pipe, to send passengers where the dead go and bring them back. I am quoting. I felt the note in my teeth."

**Tape 3 · 12 October 1983**
"Tape three. Test trolley, six volunteers, Carrier switched on. All six filled in the same form: they arrived before they left. One wrote his arrival time, then a departure time earlier than it, and did not seem troubled. I have sent a memo. Lodestar has filed it under the word anecdotal. I would like it noted that a form is not an anecdote."

**Tape 4 · 20 February 1984**
"Tape four. The Carrier is a tone we put in the rails so that ZERO can find its trains. In the old tube it comes back, not as an echo but as an answer, on Vayle's note. Two signals that agree. On a moving train I cannot tell you where that goes. I can tell you it goes first, and returns. I told them. They kept the bypass."

**Tape 5 · 9 August 1984**
"Tape five. The cars are fitted: receivers, door sensors, and a lamp-proving current that glows in a lamp the colour of a signal. The inaugural run is published. Six cars, three hundred and twelve guests, a thirteen-minute loop, home at midnight exactly. The newspapers like exactly. I have stopped saying what the loop crosses, because the next question is always whether it is on schedule."

**Tape 6 · 14 December 1984**
"Tape six. Tonight I read ZERO's log specification to the end. A run may be pending or arrived. There is no third state. The contract says no failed runs, and the engineers took that as a promise rather than a design. If 047 does not arrive, ZERO will hold it pending, and issue the departure again, and again, for as long as it has power."

**Tape 7 · 29 December 1984**
"Tape seven. I asked the chairman to postpone. He said a railway that is never late cannot start late. I asked Lodestar for grounds to refuse the run and was given a ribbon. So I will ride in the cab, with a hand on the controller. If I am right, they will all be standing on that platform, still listening to the announcement. Please find these."

**Tape 8 · 31 December 1984, 23:46, in the cab**
"Tape eight. Eleven forty-six. The band is playing; I can hear it through the glass. The champagne is still on the coupler. Nobody broke it. The chairman says they will do it on the return. Thirteen seconds. When the Carrier meets the note we will go somewhere first and be back in the same second, every clock in the station will say we never left, and the first thing I expect to notice is the"

*(The tape ends.)*

## In the map

The tapes are the main lore pickups, with text on the Tab screen: tape 1 in the Channel 9 van in the Cordon; 2 in Hole Number Nine in the Arcade; 3 on an evidence tag in the Transit Police Post; 4 on a seat of car 2 on Platform A East; 5 in the Car Barn; 6 and 7 in the Exchange; 8 on bench 4 at the Terminus.
