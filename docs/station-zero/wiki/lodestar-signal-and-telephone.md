# Lodestar Signal & Telephone Co.

**Lodestar Signal & Telephone Co.** (LS&T, "Lodestar") was founded in 1921 as a maker of telephone exchanges and by 1980 was an electronics group of 9,000 employees moving, in its own phrase, "from switching calls to switching cities". Its logo is an eight-point compass star set in a telephone dial; its advertising line is **"The Future Runs Itself."** Its headquarters stood on Calder Road in Port Halcyon.

## The contract

In March 1982 Lodestar won the contract to build the first driverless line in the country, and with it the computer to run it, [ZERO](zero.md). The contract fixed a price, an opening date of 31 December 1984 and a performance clause: "no failed runs". Lodestar's vice-president for systems, Gordon Pruitt, signed for the company and kept the clause framed in his office.

## What Lodestar knew

The company knew more than the authority. On 12 October 1983 its lead engineer, [Dr. Imogen Strand](dr-imogen-strand.md), wrote Pruitt a memo:

> "The 1893 tube answers the Carrier. Volunteers on the test trolley report arriving before leaving. Recommend the bypass be rerouted until the effect is understood."

Pruitt's reply, handwritten across the foot of the page:

> "Noted and filed. Bypass retained: saves eleven months. G.P."

The bypass was kept, and the eleven months were saved.

## The cleanup

After the Midnight Service the city awarded Lodestar the cleanup contract for the plaza. In practice this was a recovery of its own equipment. Crews wearing yellow suits with a compass-star badge went down in shifts from January 1985, working from two decontamination tents in the Cordon and eating in the old civil-defence shelter. About 140 workers went down in all. The final shift was logged on 21 June 1985, with a work order for "the core". Nobody signed out.

## Appearance

Lodestar equipment is grey, with a yellow band. Its hardware lives in the old station as grafts: yellow frames over Victorian cast iron, a grey control desk shoved in beside the old lever frame, a yellow motor bolted to Vayle's blower. Its notices are set in a compass-star serif and begin, almost invariably, with the word "Please".

## In the map

Lodestar's mark is on the Cordon's decontamination tents and trailer generator, the yellow frame over the Capsule Post hatch, the grafted switchgear in the Bellows Vault, the jammed keypad door between the Converter Hall and the Exchange, and the Exchange itself. The yellow-suited cleaner is one of the eight kinds of the dead. The "The Future Runs Itself" advertising box hangs in the Escalator Hall. A "NO HRT STAFF BEYOND THIS POINT" notice is bolted in the Staff Stair.
