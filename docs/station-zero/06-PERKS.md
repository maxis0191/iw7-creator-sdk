# 06 · PERKS

Ten machines: the six stock perks the owner named, placed with a reason, and four new ones.
Stock prices and effects are the game's own (`research/E-shipped-map-numbers.md` section 4) and are not changed.

**Perk limit on this map: six** (the stock limit is five). Reason: with ten machines and a limit of five, the
new perks would compete with the four that every player takes; with no limit, choice disappears. Six means the
usual core plus two real decisions. The Night Inspector's first Validated Ticket makes one purchase free; it
does not raise the limit. (The servers' module that removes the stock limit is off on this map through the
per-map feature profile.)

**All machines need power except Up 'N Atoms** in the Cordon, which runs off Lodestar's trailer: in the build,
the Cordon has its own generator struct switched on at match start (the stock game powers areas separately).

## 2. The four new perks

Naming follows the game's own habit: perks are sweets with a pun. Each is built on something this map has and
no other map has (the thirteen minutes, the Capsule Post and traps, the Inspector's way of marking, the photo
booth), so none could be moved to another map unchanged. The author knows of no perk or card of the base game or its
sister series with these effects; package P1 checks each against the base game's perk and card tables in the
dump before building, and a match is redesigned.

### BAKER'S DOZENS · Lodestar Exchange · 3000

- **Fantasy.** Thirteen to the bag. On this map thirteen is the number that is owed.
- **Exact effect.** A counter runs 1 to 13 on the HUD beside the ammunition. **Every thirteenth hit you land**
  (bullets that hit a zombie; misses do not count and do not reset) is **punched**: it deals triple damage,
  passes through two more zombies, and pays double hit points. The counter shows 12 in amber, so the
  thirteenth can be placed on purpose: on a head, on a Roller, on the Inspector's open lamp.
- **Does not apply to**: the four wonder weapons, launchers and explosives (their hits neither count nor punch).
- **Limit and counterplay.** One hit in thirteen is +15 % damage on average if wasted on bodies; its worth is
  entirely in where the player puts the thirteenth. Fast weapons reach it sooner but each hit is smaller.
- **Rounds.** Round 10: a nicety and a habit to learn. Round 25: a marksman who saves the thirteenth for heads
  gets a guaranteed kill every thirteen hits. Round 40: it keeps bullet weapons useful against the Inspector's
  lamp; it cannot touch the weapons that carry round 40.
- **Machine.** A doughnut-shop counter dispenser in cream and red enamel: a glass drum of sugared jelly drops,
  a paper bag chute, a mechanical counter with flip digits that runs 01 to 13 and rings on 13.
- **Jingle idea.** A counting song in swing time, a baker's bell on every thirteenth beat: "…eleven, twelve,
  and one for luck".
- **Icon idea.** A paper bag with "13" and one drop falling out of it, the drop in amber.

### BON VOYAGES · the Parlour · 2000

- **Fantasy.** Travel sweets from the tin, for the passenger who goes everywhere.
- **Exact effect.** For you: **postage on the Capsule Post is half price**; you arrive with your weapon already up;
  after any arrival (Capsule Post or the 23:47's return) you move 20 % faster for 4 s; **traps you buy cost
  half**, and a trap you bought does not hurt you.
- **Limit and counterplay.** It adds no damage and no health. The Post still has two capsules per hatch and
  still blows zombies in behind you; the lever still decides what is near. It is the perk of the player who
  runs the map for the team: opens hatches, fires traps, fetches parts.
- **Rounds.** Round 10: saves a few thousand points of postage and traps while the team is still opening the
  map. Round 25: the trap discount is real money (four traps at 375). Round 40: the only perk that makes
  repositioning free, which is how late rounds are survived; it still kills nothing.
- **Machine.** A walnut and brass Atmospheric ticket cabinet turned sweet dispenser: round tins of dusted
  travel sweets behind a brass grille, a destination dial on the front that spins when you buy, gilt letters
  "FOR THE JOURNEY".
- **Jingle idea.** A brisk music-hall patter on a harmonium, a guard's "all aboard" call, a tin lid snapping shut.
- **Icon idea.** A round tin seen from above with a dial pointer on it.

### SOUR GRUDGES · the Boneyard · 2500

- **Fantasy.** Sour sweets for people who do not forgive.
- **Exact effect.** The zombie that last hit you is **marked for 6 s** (a green fume over its head, seen only by
  you). Kill it yourself inside the 6 s with an ordinary weapon: **the next hit you take within 8 s does no
  damage** (the screen edge shows a green rim while this holds). One mark at a time; one forgiven hit at a time.
- **Does not apply to**: the Night Inspector and the Rollers (no mark); kills made with a wonder weapon.
- **Limit and counterplay.** It gives nothing back: the first hit still landed. It turns a good reaction into
  one hit of cover, and only against one hit: two zombies swinging together still take health.
- **Rounds.** Round 10: a good shot is nearly always covered against single hits. Round 25: killing one
  particular zombie in 6 s with a bullet weapon takes aim and Pack-a-Punch; half the marks expire. Round 40:
  rarely pays; it scales down by itself, which a flat health perk does not.
- **Machine.** A crooked green and yellow gumball machine with a scowl painted over the maker's smile by the
  Zero Kings; the globe is full of puckered sour drops; a crown stencil on the base.
- **Jingle idea.** A playground taunt on a cheap organ, sung too sweetly; it ends with a raspberry.
- **Icon idea.** A puckered face sweet with crossed-out eyes and a small green cloud.

### DOUBLE TAKES · Underpass Arcade, at the photo booth · 3000

- **Fantasy.** The photo-booth sweet: one flash, and there are two of you.
- **Exact effect.** When your health falls **below 35 %**, a flash goes off and a **still image of you**
  stays where you stood for **4 s**. Zombies within **400 units** go for the image (engine zombies and crowd
  bodies alike: the image is a target in the crowd's flow field). You move 20 % faster for 1.5 s.
  **Cooldown 60 s**; the icon is dim while it recharges.
- **Does not apply to**: the Night Inspector and the Rollers (they pass through the image).
- **Limit and counterplay.** A blow that takes you from above 35 % straight to zero skips it. Zombies farther
  than 400 units keep coming. Once a minute.
- **Rounds.** Round 10: saves a careless player once a minute. Round 25: it triggers only if a hit leaves the
  player alive under 35 %; how often that happens depends on what a zombie's hit does at that round, which the
  measuring helper could not read from the scripts: P1 measures it and, if two-hit deaths make the perk dead
  without Tuff 'Nuff, the threshold becomes "the first hit taken after 10 s unhurt". Then it buys four
  seconds from the nearest dozen. Round 40: with 78 alive the image empties a circle and the
  ring outside it closes: an opening for one dash, not an escape.
- **Machine.** A photo-booth-shaped dispenser in turquoise and chrome with a curtain, a flash bulb on top that
  pops when you buy, and a slot that delivers a strip of four identical sweets.
- **Jingle idea.** A camera-shop radio ad: a bright vocal duo, every line sung twice, a flash "pof" on the beat.
- **Icon idea.** Two overlapping head silhouettes, the rear one outlined in dashes.

## 3. Strong, not broken: the check against each other

| | Adds damage | Adds survival | Adds economy or movement | Scales with round |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baker's Dozens | one placed hit in thirteen, bullet weapons only | no | double points on that hit | level (skill decides) |
| Bon Voyages | no | no | half-price postage and traps, a burst of speed on arrival | up in usefulness, never in power |
| Sour Grudges | no | one forgiven hit, if earned | no | down |
| Double Takes | no | one four-second opening a minute | no | down |

No new perk multiplies another or a wonder weapon: Baker's Dozens and Sour Grudges ignore wonder weapons; Day
Trippers touches no combat number; Double Takes cannot trigger while it recharges. Named test cases for P1:
Sour Grudges with each wonder weapon (must not trigger); Double Takes inside a Live Rail triangle and on a
slick; Bon Voyages with eight players queuing at one hatch; Baker's Dozens on the Inspector's lamp.
A player with all four and two stock perks has given up two of Tuff 'Nuff, Quickies, Bang Bangs and Racin'
Stripes: the limit of six is the real balance.
